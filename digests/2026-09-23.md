## New developments

### Regulatory

**USDA Sends Mandatory Dairy Manufacturing Cost Survey Proposed Rule to OMB for Review** (Medium · Monitor Only) — USDA's Agricultural Marketing Service submitted a Proposed Rule, "Dairy Program — Mandatory Manufacturing Cost Survey" (RIN 0581-AE47), to OMB's Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs on Sept. 21, 2026, where it is listed as pending review and classified economically significant. The rule would implement the mandatory dairy-processing cost and product-yield survey authorized by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025, following an advance notice and comment period that closed in March 2026. *Why it matters:* The rule would require dairy processing plants that report prices under the Dairy Products Mandatory Reporting Program to disclose detailed manufacturing cost and yield data to USDA, feeding directly into Federal Milk Marketing Order make-allowance calculations; IDFA has broadly supported the concept, but the specific reporting, verification and confidentiality mechanics have not yet been released. OIRA review is typically the last step before Federal Register publication and a public comment period. [OMB/OIRA — reginfo.gov](https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/eoReviewSearch), [Federal Register (related ANPRM)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/27/2026-04013/mandatory-manufacturing-cost-survey-advanced-notice-of-proposed-rulemaking)
