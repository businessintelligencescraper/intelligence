## New developments

### Regulatory

**U.S.-China Board of Trade Formalizes Procedures, Names Dairy Among Products for Possible Tariff Relief** (Low · Monitor Only) — The U.S. and China issued formal working procedures for the newly created U.S.-China Board of Trade, setting up a dialogue mechanism to consider reciprocal reduced-tariff treatment on roughly $30 billion of goods each side. The initial product list includes dairy categories — cheese, butter, yogurt, and whey/modified whey. *Why it matters:* First concrete procedural step toward possibly easing China's retaliatory tariffs on US dairy exports, but no actual tariff reduction has occurred yet. [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/global-trade-tariffs/2026/10/01/us-china-establish-trade-board-will-consider-reduced-tariffs-on-us-dairy-exports/)

### Legislative

**NAW's Oregon Packaging-EPR Appeal Formally Docketed at Ninth Circuit; Briefing Schedule Set** (Low · Monitor Only) — The National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors' appeal of the ruling upholding Oregon's packaging EPR law has been docketed at the Ninth Circuit as NAW v. Feldon, No. 26-6404, with NAW's opening brief due Dec. 17, 2026 and Oregon's response due Jan. 18, 2027. *Why it matters:* Sets the timeline for the next phase of the first full federal trial testing the constitutionality of a state packaging-EPR law, a bellwether for programs in all seven states that have enacted them. [Resource Recycling](http://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/25/naw-appeals-oregon-epr-ruling/), [The National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ninth-circuit-review-constitutional-challenge-oregons-epr-law)

### Dairy Industry

**IDFA's Top Regulatory Affairs Executive Announces Retirement; Successor Named** (Low · Monitor Only) — Roberta Wagner, IDFA's senior vice president of regulatory and scientific affairs, announced she will retire effective Feb. 15, 2027, after more than four decades across FDA, USDA-FSIS and industry roles. Dr. Samuel Alcaine will succeed her in the role, and Michelle Albee Matto was promoted to vice president, regulatory affairs and chief nutrition scientist, effective immediately. *Why it matters:* Wagner has been a key industry-side contact on FDA/USDA dairy regulatory matters (standards of identity, GRAS, UPF definition); a multi-month transition period in IDFA's top regulatory role is worth tracking for continuity on active dockets, though it is a routine personnel transition with no immediate policy effect. [Perishable News](https://perishablenews.com/dairy/idfa-senior-vice-president-roberta-wagner-announces-retirement-samuel-alcaine-to-succeed-wagner/), [RRFN](https://www.rrfn.com/2026/10/02/idfas-wagner-announces-retirement-plan/)

**Correction: FDA's Newest Listeria Investigation Not Confirmed Linked to Sierra Nevada Raw-Milk Cheese** (Medium · Monitor Only) — Follow-up food-safety press coverage of the Listeria monocytogenes investigation FDA opened around Sept. 30 (11 illnesses) reports that FDA has named no food, company or state for that investigation — contradicting the circumstantial link to Sierra Nevada Graziers raw-milk cheese reported in earlier coverage. The separate, confirmed E. coli O26 outbreak tied to Sierra Nevada Graziers cheese (13 illnesses, 8 hospitalized, 3 with hemolytic uremic syndrome) is unchanged and remains under its own nationwide recall. *Why it matters:* Narrows the scope of the raw-milk-cheese outbreak story — rather than two pathogens compounding on the same recalled product, the newer Listeria investigation now appears to be a separate, unattributed event, somewhat reducing the urgency case for the pending FDA citizen petition on raw-milk-cheese warning labels. [Food Poison Journal](https://www.foodpoisonjournal.com/food-poisoning-information/fda-opened-a-new-listeria-outbreak-investigation-on-september-30-2026-11-sick-no-food-named-no-states-listed-here-is-what-pregnant-women-older-adults-and-everyone-else-should-know/), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/10/new-listeria-outbreak-under-investigation/)
