**U.S. Dairy Processing Investment Has Reached $13 Billion Over the Past Year, NMPF CEO Says** (Low · Monitor Only) — National Milk Producers Federation CEO Gregg Doud said U.S. dairy processors have invested roughly $13 billion in new and expanded manufacturing capacity over the past year, pointing to examples including Chobani's New York and Idaho expansions, Fairlife, and new Hilmar Cheese capacity in southwest Kansas or Texas. Doud attributed the wave to rising milk production while flagging longer-term questions about logistics and transportation capacity. *Why it matters:* A sustained, sector-wide processing build-out reshapes competitive dynamics, plant siting decisions, and the logistics/labor landscape the company operates in — useful context for capacity and market-share planning, with no near-term regulatory or reputational exposure. [Brownfield Ag News](https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/news/13-billion-in-dairy-processing-investment-reshapes-u-s-industry/)

**CoBank Report: U.S. Dairy Industry Accelerates Push to Raise Milk Protein Levels** (Low · Monitor Only) — A CoBank Knowledge Exchange report distributed Aug. 27 finds U.S. milk protein production has begun outpacing butterfat growth as milk component pricing incentives shift toward protein, though the U.S. still trails the EU and New Zealand on overall protein content. The report frames a more balanced protein/butterfat composition as necessary to keep U.S. milk competitive with leading dairy exporters and to support domestic cheesemaking. *Why it matters:* Protein-to-butterfat composition directly affects cheese yield economics and ingredient formulation; a sustained compositional shift in the milk supply changes input costs and sourcing/formulation planning for any U.S. dairy processor. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/27/3352319/0/en/u-s-dairy-industry-accelerates-push-to-boost-milk-protein-levels.html)
