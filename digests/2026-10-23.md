## ⚠ Priority items

**New Jersey cheese importer sues to block Trump's 50% Section 338 tariffs on Canadian goods** — Gellert Global Group Corp., a $1.7B New Jersey food importer specializing in Canadian cheese (brie, camembert, goat cheese), filed suit Oct. 20, 2026 in the U.S. Court of International Trade (represented by the Liberty Justice Center), the first direct legal challenge to the Section 338 tariffs that have applied 50% duties to Canadian-origin dairy ingredients, alcohol and autos since the summer. The suit argues the statute was effectively superseded by Section 301 procedures, that the administration skipped a required 30-day notice period, and that the 50% rate isn't tied to any measured burden on US commerce; it seeks to strike the tariffs, halt collection and refund duties, on behalf of a proposed class of importers.
*Why it matters:* This is the first court test of the tariff authority underlying the Canadian dairy-ingredient tariffs that have been shaping sourcing costs across the industry since August; a plaintiff win could invalidate the tariffs and trigger refunds, while a loss cements the administration's approach.
*Recommended action:* Brief leadership that the Section 338 tariffs are now under direct legal challenge; monitor the Court of International Trade docket for a preliminary-injunction motion or scheduling order.
