**Canada, US Trade Ministers Hold Fifth Meeting on Tariff Deadline; No Deal Reached** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required) — Canadian Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc and US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer held a one-hour virtual meeting Sunday, Aug. 16 — their fifth since Trump's tariff threat — which LeBlanc's office described as "constructive," saying the two sides "took stock" of negotiating-team progress and that discussions are continuing.
