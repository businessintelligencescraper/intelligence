**North Dakota Voters Narrowly Reject Universal Free School Meals Amendment** (Low · Monitor Only) — North Dakota voters rejected Measure 2 on Nov. 3, 2026, a constitutional amendment that would have required public schools to provide a free breakfast and lunch to every student starting with the 2027-28 school year, funded through the state's Legacy Fund. The measure failed by a narrow margin, roughly 50.6% to 49.4%. *Why it matters:* Universal free school meal programs expand guaranteed volume for school milk and dairy purchasing; this measure's defeat means North Dakota's school nutrition funding and procurement stay unchanged for now. Advocates and some legislators have signaled they will pursue the issue again in the 2027 legislative session, but no bill or date has been set.
