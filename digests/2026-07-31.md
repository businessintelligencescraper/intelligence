### Nutrition

**Harvard Study of 200,000 Adults Finds Overall Diet Quality, Not Processing Level, Drives Health Outcomes** (Medium · Potential Media Interest) — Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health researchers presented a four-decade analysis of more than 200,000 U.S. adults at the American Society for Nutrition's NUTRITION 2026 conference on July 28, finding that overall nutritional quality — not the degree of industrial processing — was the dominant driver of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and mortality risk. The findings are a conference abstract and have not yet undergone peer review.
*Why it matters:* One of the largest studies to date directly challenges the blanket "ultra-processed = unhealthy" framing underlying pending FDA front-of-pack labeling and the Senate's UPF warning-label bill; it gives dairy communicators credible science to cite when defending processed dairy formats against categorical UPF criticism, but its preliminary, non-peer-reviewed status means it should be used cautiously. [EurekAlert! (American Society for Nutrition release)](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1136477), [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322054/20260729/ultra-processed-food-labels-steer-diets-wrong-harvard-study-200000-finds.htm)

### Regulatory

**E. Coli Outbreak Tied to Frozen Organic Blueberries Widens to Full Publix Recall** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA and CDC are investigating a multistate E. coli O145:H28 outbreak linked to frozen GreenWise-brand organic blueberries and mixed berries from Chilean supplier Frutas y Hortalizas del Sur S.A. A single-lot recall issued July 3 widened on July 29 when Publix pulled all lots of the affected products after 12 illnesses were confirmed across two states.
*Why it matters:* Not a dairy product, but another cold-chain/frozen-food recall drawing FDA, CDC, and retailer attention keeps foodborne-illness response and recall readiness in the news cycle; no direct exposure to the company's dairy portfolio. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/publix-recalls-all-lots-greenwise-organic-frozen-blueberries-and-whole-mixed-berries-due-potential-e), [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/ecoli/outbreaks/blueberries-07-26/index.html), [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/health/food-recalls/frozen-blueberries-e-coli-publix-a1312287969/)
