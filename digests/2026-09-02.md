### Regulatory
**USDA Publishes Annual Dairy Import-Licensing Notices for the 2027 Quota Year** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA's Foreign Agricultural Service published two routine Federal Register notices on Sept. 2 governing the Dairy Tariff-Rate Quota (TRQ) import licensing program: one sets the 2027 license fee at $325 per license (total program cost estimated at $631,516 across roughly 1,950 licenses), and the other shifts specified quota amounts for certain dairy articles from the historical license category to the lottery (nonhistorical) category for the 2027 quota year. *Why it matters:* These are routine annual administrative filings, not a policy change, but they set the mechanics — fees and license-category allocations — for who can import quota-limited dairy ingredients in 2027. Worth a line in the trade-policy file given the active, high-profile US-Canada dairy tariff dispute already on the company's radar.
