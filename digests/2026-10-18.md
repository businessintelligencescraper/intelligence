### Dairy Industry

**Family Farmstead Dairy Recalls Raw Cheddar Cheese Over E. coli Contamination** (Medium · Potential Media Interest) — Family Farmstead Dairy of Worcester, New York voluntarily recalled one lot of its Raw Mild Cheddar Cheese (A2 Raw Cheddar, lot/best-by code 7/22/27) on October 15 after product testing detected Shiga toxin-producing E. coli. The cheese was distributed only to grocers in New York and New Jersey; the company says it has received no reports of illness and no other Family Farmstead products are affected.
*Why it matters:* This is the latest in a string of raw-milk cheese safety incidents this year, following the Sierra Nevada Graziers E. coli/Listeria outbreaks and the AAP's renewed call to ban raw milk sales. Though small in scale, swiftly recalled and linked to no illnesses, it adds another data point to the pattern advocates are citing in pushing for category-wide raw-milk cheese warning labeling.
