### Regulatory

**FDA Finalizes Rule Allowing Salt Substitutes in 139 Standardized Foods, Including Cheese and Sour Cream** (Low · Potential Media Interest) — FDA published a final rule in the Federal Register on Oct. 13, 2026 amending 139 food standards of identity — including dairy standards covering cheeses and sour cream — to permit salt substitutes alongside or in place of required/permitted salt. Previously only margarine and low-sodium Cheddar/Colby cheese were allowed substitutes; the rule defines a substitute by function rather than naming approved ingredients, and carries no mandate to reformulate.
*Why it matters:* This directly answers a multi-year industry ask to extend salt-substitute flexibility into standardized dairy products, giving cheese and sour cream makers a reformulation path to cut sodium without losing standard-of-identity compliance; both industry and public-health advocates welcomed it, a rare alignment that lowers reputational risk.
