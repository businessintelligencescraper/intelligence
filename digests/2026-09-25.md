**Conservative Social-Media Boycott Targets Tillamook Over Halal-Certified Cheese Labeling** — Starting around Sept. 18, 2026, conservative social-media accounts launched a boycott campaign against Tillamook, an Oregon dairy cooperative, after users noticed a "Certified Halal" mark on its cheese packaging, with some posts calling the products "Al Qaeda cheese." **Wholesaler-Distributor Trade Group Appeals Oregon Packaging-EPR Ruling to the Ninth Circuit** (Low · Monitor Only) — The National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors (NAW) filed a notice of appeal on Sept. 24, 2026 with the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, challenging Judge Michael Simon's Aug. 27, 2026 ruling that upheld Oregon's Plastic Pollution and Recycling Modernization Act (the state's packaging extended producer responsibility law) against NAW's Dormant Commerce Clause and Due Process challenges. NAW President and CEO Eric Hoplin said the trial record still shows the law is unconstitutional and asked the appeals court to reverse the district court's legal conclusions, including on the delegation of fee-setting authority to Circular Action Alliance. *Why it matters:* This extends the first full federal trial testing packaging-EPR constitutionality into a second court, keeping litigation risk live across all seven states with enacted EPR packaging laws (CA, CO, MD, ME, MN, OR, WA) that touch a national dairy manufacturer's packaging compliance; Oregon's law remains in effect and enforceable while the appeal proceeds, so there is no near-term compliance change. [NAW](https://www.naw.org/naw-appeals-oregon-epr-ruling-ninth-circuit/)

### Sustainability

**California Governor Vetoes Bill Directing SB 54 Plastic Pollution Fund Spending** (Low · Monitor Only) — Gov. Gavin Newsom on Sept. 21, 2026 vetoed SB 1180 (Sen. Benjamin Allen), which would have set spending priorities and eligible uses for the $5 billion, 10-year Plastic Pollution Mitigation Fund created under California's SB 54 packaging producer-responsibility law. Newsom said the bill "risks complicating implementation" of SB 54 and the fund while his administration works through the law's core producer-responsibility rollout; industry groups including the Flexible Packaging Association and Foodservice Packaging Institute had opposed the bill, while environmental and tribal advocates had supported it. *Why it matters:* SB 54 is the country's most comprehensive packaging EPR law and applies to any packaging a national dairy manufacturer places on the California market; keeping the fund's spending rules unsettled for now avoids a new compliance-adjacent mandate but leaves open how mitigation dollars will eventually be allocated. [Recycling Today](https://www.recyclingtoday.com/news/california-governor-gavin-newsom-vetoes-bill-outlining-use-of-sb-54-plastic-pollution-fund/), [Resource Recycling](http://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/24/newsom-signs-battery-epr-expansion-vetoes-sb-54-fund-bill/)
