**IDFA Names Matt Herrick Next President and CEO, Succeeding Michael Dykes** (Low · Monitor Only) — IDFA's Executive Council selected Matt Herrick, currently president and CEO of the International Bottled Water Association and a seven-year IDFA veteran, as the association's next president and CEO. *Why it matters:* IDFA is the primary national trade association representing US dairy processors and manufacturers on labeling, GRAS, tariff and nutrition-policy fights. A change at the top of that advocacy relationship is worth tracking as the new CEO sets priorities for 2027, though it is a routine, well-managed succession.
