### Legislative

**NAW Asks Oregon Court to Pause Its Own Packaging-EPR Fee Ruling Pending Appeal** (Low · Monitor Only) — The National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors (NAW) filed a motion Oct. 19 asking the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon to stay its own Aug. 27 ruling — which upheld Oregon's packaging extended producer responsibility (EPR) law and lifted NAW's earlier preliminary injunction — while NAW's appeal is pending before the Ninth Circuit. The American Forest & Paper Association has filed a similar request.
*Why it matters:* A ruling matters before January 2027, when Oregon's EPR administrator plans to send member companies their next round of compliance invoices; if the stay is granted it would restore the fee exemption NAW members had from February through August, and if denied, members must decide whether to pay invoices they may not get refunded even if the law is later struck down on appeal.
*Recommended action:* Continue monitoring; no change to current Oregon EPR payment obligations until the court rules on the stay motion. [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/10/20/naw-asks-to-pause-oregon-epr-fees/)

### Dairy Industry

**IDFA Names Matt Herrick Next President and CEO, Succeeding Michael Dykes** (Low · Monitor Only) — IDFA's Executive Council selected Matt Herrick, currently president and CEO of the International Bottled Water Association and a seven-year IDFA veteran, as the association's next president and CEO. He succeeds Michael Dykes, who is retiring after a decade leading IDFA; Herrick takes over Jan. 1, 2027, with Dykes staying through Jan. 31 to assist the transition.
*Why it matters:* IDFA is the primary national trade association representing US dairy processors and manufacturers on labeling, GRAS, tariff and nutrition-policy fights. A change at the top of that advocacy relationship is worth tracking as the new CEO sets priorities for 2027, though it is a routine, well-managed succession.
