### Nutrition

**New Audit Finds Over 80% of Toddler Foods on Store Shelves Are Ultra-Processed** (Medium · Monitor Only) — Researchers presenting at the Nutrition 2026 conference audited 2,783 foods marketed to children ages 6-36 months in Austin, TX grocery stores and found more than 80% qualify as ultra-processed; nearly half failed at least one WHO nutrition standard for children's food, most often on sugar content. *Why it matters:* Keeps ultra-processed foods squarely in the spotlight for products marketed to families, a category the company competes in through yogurt and dairy snack products; reinforces the value of a recognizable-ingredient, minimally-processed positioning as UPF scrutiny continues to build toward possible labeling or procurement policy. ### Sustainability

**EU Packaging Rule's PFAS Ban and Producer-Responsibility Requirements Start the Clock for US Exporters** (Low · Monitor Only) — Trade press this week reminded food exporters that the EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation becomes applicable August 12, 2026, banning PFAS above set thresholds in food-contact packaging and adding conformity, minimization, and extended-producer-responsibility requirements on any packaging placed on the EU market regardless of origin, with no transition period for noncompliant stock.
*Why it matters:* Any product or packaging component the company ships into the EU would need to meet the PFAS thresholds and PPWR requirements by the mid-August deadline; worth a quick confirmation with supply chain/packaging teams if EU-bound volume exists, though most of the company's output is domestic. [FreshFruitPortal](https://www.freshfruitportal.com/news/2026/07/27/eu-produce-packaging/), [Food Safety Magazine](https://www.food-safety.com/articles/11228-pfas-free-food-packaging-by-august-2026)

### Public Affairs

**Multistate Salmonella Outbreak Tied to Recalled Eggs Grows to 17 States** (Medium · Monitor Only) — CDC's investigation update confirmed the Salmonella Enteritidis outbreak linked to Midwest Poultry Services shell eggs has reached at least 98 illnesses and 26 hospitalizations across 17 states, with Georgia added as of July 27. The recall, issued July 22 for roughly 1.59 million dozen eggs, affects Kroger, Brookshire's, and several private-label brands.
*Why it matters:* A fast-spreading, multi-retailer recall shows how quickly a contamination event can escalate into sustained national coverage and CDC/FDA joint action; while eggs are outside the company's category, the case is a useful live benchmark for recall-response speed, retailer communication, and outbreak-tracking cadence. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/salmonella/outbreaks/shell-eggs-07-26/index.html), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/07/recalled-eggs-linked-to-salmonella-outbreak-spanning-17-states/), [News4JAX](https://www.news4jax.com/news/local/2026/07/27/salmonella-outbreak-tied-to-recalled-eggs-spreads-to-georgia/)
