**US-Canada Trade Negotiators Meet in Washington as Trump, Carney Voice Optimism on Tariff Deal** — US and Canadian trade officials met in person in Washington Aug. 19 to continue negotiating the pending deal that paused 50% Section 338 tariffs on Canadian-origin dairy ingredients, alcohol and vehicles. ### Nutrition

**Trade Press Flags New Animal Study Linking Xanthan Gum to Gut Inflammation** (Low · Monitor Only) — FoodNavigator reported Aug. 19 on a peer-reviewed rat study (Federal University of Sao Paulo, published in PLOS One) finding that chronic xanthan gum consumption caused colon inflammation, disrupted intestinal barrier integrity, elevated inflammatory markers and altered gut microbiota, with effects strongest at medium and high doses. Xanthan gum is a common stabilizer/thickener used in thousands of food and beverage products. *Why it matters:* Xanthan gum is used as a stabilizer in some dairy products (yogurt, cottage cheese, dairy-based dressings and drinks); while this is an early-stage rodent study rather than human data, it lands amid heightened scrutiny of food additives and could feed the same additive-disclosure and clean-label pressure already driving pending GRAS-disclosure legislation. [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/08/19/xanthan-gum-faces-scrutiny-despite-use-in-thousands-of-food-and-beverage-products/)
