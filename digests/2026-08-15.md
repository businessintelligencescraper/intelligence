### Dairy Industry

**Saputo Agrees to Sell UK Dairy Division to Lactalis for £988 Million** (Low · Monitor Only) — Canadian dairy processor Saputo announced Aug. 14 a definitive agreement to sell its UK Dairy Division — five manufacturing facilities and brands including Cathedral City, Wensleydale, Davidstow, Clover and Country Life — to France's Lactalis at an enterprise value of about £988 million (~US$1.86 billion). The UK unit generated roughly US$1.2 billion in revenue over the past four quarters, about 7% of Saputo's consolidated total; closing is expected by the end of Q1 calendar 2027 pending regulatory approval.
*Why it matters:* Consolidates two of the dairy sector's largest global processors and signals continued geography-driven portfolio rationalization rather than category exit; no direct US regulatory or reputational exposure, but a useful data point on industry consolidation trends for competitive-landscape tracking. [Saputo (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/14/3345243/0/en/saputo-enters-agreement-to-sell-its-united-kingdom-operations.html), [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2026/08/14/saputo-to-sell-uk-operations-to-lactalis-for-around-186-billion/), [Food Manufacture](https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2026/08/14/cathedral-city-and-clover-owner-sells-off-uk-dairy-operations/)

**USDA Awards Dairy Purchase Contracts for Cheese, Yogurt and Milk; Receives No Butter Offers** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA awarded contracts Aug. 14 under its regular commodity purchase program for cheese (Associated Milk Producers, Great Lakes Cheese, Masters Gallery Foods, Miceli Dairy Products, Sargento Foods), high-protein yogurt (Chobani), and UHT/evaporated milk (California Dairies, Diversified Foods, Gossner Foods, Naturally Brand, Upstate Niagara), but received no offers for the 102,600 lbs of butter it sought, so no butter was purchased.
*Why it matters:* Routine government purchase activity, but the no-offer outcome on butter is a small signal on current butter supply/pricing dynamics worth noting for market context; no direct action implication. [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/production-supply-chain/2026/08/14/usda-awards-contracts-for-cheese-yogurt-milk-products-gets-no-butter-offers/)

**USDA's August WASDE Report Holds 2026 Milk Production Forecast Flat, Trims 2027 Outlook** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA's August World Agricultural Supply and Demand Estimates report, covered by Cheese Reporter Aug. 14, left the 2026 U.S. milk production forecast unchanged at 236.6 billion lbs. (4.9 billion lbs. above 2025's record output) while cutting the 2027 forecast by 100 million lbs. to 238.0 billion lbs. Dairy product price forecasts were mixed: 2026 butter and nonfat dry milk price forecasts were lowered, while cheese and dry whey forecasts were raised.
*Why it matters:* Routine but closely watched monthly market signal for the dairy sector's supply and pricing trajectory; the mixed product-price revisions are relevant context for margin and product-mix planning, though no comms action is required. [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/production-supply-chain/2026/08/14/dairy-product-milk-price-forecasts-mixed-in-latest-usda-projections/)

### Food Industry

**FDA Assigns Class I Risk to Midwest Poultry Services Egg Recall Tied to Salmonella Outbreak** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA classified as Class I (highest risk) the July 22 recall of 1,589,577 dozen (about 19 million) white and cage-free brown shell eggs produced in Texas by Midwest Poultry Services, L.P. for possible Salmonella Enteritidis contamination; the eggs were sold under Country Morning, Cal-Maine Sunups, Brookshire's, Simple Truth and Kroger brand names. CDC reports 98 illnesses and 26 hospitalizations across 17 states, with no deaths, in the linked multistate outbreak.
*Why it matters:* Not a dairy product and outside the company's own supply chain, but a Class I, multistate egg-salmonella recall is another live benchmark this year for recall scope, retailer private-label exposure (Kroger, Simple Truth), and FDA/CDC coordination — relevant to how the company would manage a comparable dairy-ingredient event. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/midwest-poultry-services-lp-recalls-shell-eggs-due-possible-salmonella-enteritidis-contamination), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/health/2026/08/14/15-million-dozen-recalled-eggs-receive-fdas-highest-risk-warning/)
