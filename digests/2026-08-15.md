### Dairy Industry

**Saputo Agrees to Sell UK Dairy Division to Lactalis for £988 Million** (Low · Monitor Only) — Canadian dairy processor Saputo announced Aug. 14 a definitive agreement to sell its UK Dairy Division — five manufacturing facilities and brands including Cathedral City, Wensleydale, Davidstow, Clover and Country Life — to France's Lactalis at an enterprise value of about £988 million (~US$1.86 billion). The UK unit generated roughly US$1.2 billion in revenue over the past four quarters, about 7% of Saputo's consolidated total; closing is expected by the end of Q1 calendar 2027 pending regulatory approval.
*Why it matters:* Consolidates two of the dairy sector's largest global processors and signals continued geography-driven portfolio rationalization rather than category exit; no direct US regulatory or reputational exposure, but a useful data point on industry consolidation trends for competitive-landscape tracking. **USDA Awards Dairy Purchase Contracts for Cheese, Yogurt and Milk; Receives No Butter Offers** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA awarded contracts Aug. 14 under its regular commodity purchase program for cheese (Associated Milk Producers, Great Lakes Cheese, Masters Gallery Foods, Miceli Dairy Products, Sargento Foods), high-protein yogurt (Chobani), and UHT/evaporated milk (California Dairies, Diversified Foods, Gossner Foods, Naturally Brand, Upstate Niagara), but received no offers for the 102,600 lbs of butter it sought, so no butter was purchased.
*Why it matters:* Routine government purchase activity, but the no-offer outcome on butter is a small signal on current butter supply/pricing dynamics worth noting for market context; no direct action implication.
