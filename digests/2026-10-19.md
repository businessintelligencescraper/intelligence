**Colorado Moves to Partly Dismiss Wholesalers' Challenge to Its Packaging EPR Law** (Low · Monitor Only) — Colorado's health department (CDPHE) filed a motion on Oct. 13, 2026 to dismiss two of the constitutional claims in the National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors' lawsuit over the state's packaging extended producer responsibility law (NAW v. Ryan, No. 1:26-cv-03460). The state argues NAW's private-nondelegation and unconstitutional-conditions theories already failed when it raised them against Oregon's EPR law earlier this year. The motion leaves NAW's Dormant Commerce Clause and First Amendment claims, and the separate still-pending preliminary-injunction motion, untouched. *Why it matters:* Colorado is the third state EPR program NAW has challenged after Oregon and California; a partial win for the state here would narrow but not resolve the litigation overhang facing all seven states with packaging EPR laws, several of which cover a national dairy manufacturer's packaging. Current reporting and dues obligations are unchanged either way. [Foley & Lardner](https://www.foley.com/?p=130154)
