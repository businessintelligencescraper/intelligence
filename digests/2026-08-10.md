## New developments

**USDA FSIS Issues Public Health Alert for Meat and Poultry Products Containing Recalled Jalapeños** (Low · Monitor Only) — FSIS on Aug. 8 issued a public health alert covering roughly 18 meat and poultry products — sold under retailer brands including Walmart's Marketside, Kroger, H-E-B, Albertsons, Randalls, Tom Thumb, Hannaford, RaceTrac and Wawa across more than 20 states — made with FDA-regulated jalapeños tied to the Salmonella outbreak traced to Coast Citrus Distributors' Sinaloa, Mexico-grown peppers. No illnesses have been confirmed from the FSIS-regulated products themselves; the underlying outbreak has sickened 345 people across 27 states. *Why it matters:* Not a dairy product and no company exposure, but the alert illustrates how a single contaminated produce ingredient can cascade into recalls across multiple manufacturers, retailers and food categories — a useful reference point for any packaged-food producer's ingredient-traceability and supplier-verification programs.
