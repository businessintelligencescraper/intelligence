## New developments

### Regulatory

**HHS Secretary Publicly Announces GRAS Notification Overhaul, Confirms UPF Definition Advancing** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required) — HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. publicly announced FDA's proposed rule requiring mandatory notification when manufacturers self-determine a food ingredient is "Generally Recognized as Safe" (GRAS), ending the voluntary-only system in place since 1958; Federal Register publication remains on track for December 2026. HHS and USDA also confirmed the government's first proposed ultra-processed food definition has been submitted for final review, though its content hasn't been released. *Why it matters:* This is the public unveiling of a policy already on the watch list after it cleared OMB review in July — CNN, CNBC, AP and other national outlets covered it today, moving a quiet regulatory-process story into active mainstream news that could prompt press outreach to ingredient suppliers and dairy manufacturers. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/10/health/fda-food-ingredients-gras-wellness), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/hhs-makes-announcements-on-gras-ultraprocessed-food.html)

### Food Industry

**USDA FSIS Issues Public Health Alert for Meat and Poultry Products Containing Recalled Jalapeños** (Low · Monitor Only) — FSIS on Aug. 8 issued a public health alert covering roughly 18 meat and poultry products — sold under retailer brands including Walmart's Marketside, Kroger, H-E-B, Albertsons, Randalls, Tom Thumb, Hannaford, RaceTrac and Wawa across more than 20 states — made with FDA-regulated jalapeños tied to the Salmonella outbreak traced to Coast Citrus Distributors' Sinaloa, Mexico-grown peppers. No illnesses have been confirmed from the FSIS-regulated products themselves; the underlying outbreak has sickened 345 people across 27 states. *Why it matters:* Not a dairy product and no company exposure, but the alert illustrates how a single contaminated produce ingredient can cascade into recalls across multiple manufacturers, retailers and food categories — a useful reference point for any packaged-food producer's ingredient-traceability and supplier-verification programs. [USDA FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/fsis-issues-public-health-alert-various-meat-and-poultry-products-containing-fda-0), [WGCU News](https://www.wgcu.org/health/2026-08-09/public-health-alert-for-various-meat-and-poultry-products-containing-recalled-jalapenos)
