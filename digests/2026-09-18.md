### Dairy Industry

**CoBank Analysis: Ten States Now Produce 74% of US Milk as Production Consolidates** (Low · Monitor Only) — A Sept. 17, 2026 CoBank analysis by lead dairy economist Corey Geiger finds that 10 states — California, Wisconsin, Idaho, Texas, New York, Michigan, Minnesota, Pennsylvania, Iowa and New Mexico — now produce 74% of US milk (84% among the top 14), with growth concentrated in states with available water and new processing investment. Since January 2023, Texas, Idaho, Kansas, South Dakota and Michigan have added dairy cows while Wisconsin's herd has shrunk by about 44,000 head. *Why it matters:* Continued geographic consolidation of US milk supply toward the Great Plains, Southwest and parts of the Upper Midwest has direct implications for a national dairy manufacturer's sourcing, plant-siting, water-risk and logistics planning as new processing capacity follows the milk.
