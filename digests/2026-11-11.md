# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Daily Brief — November 11, 2026

## New developments

### Regulatory
**FDA Holds Second Public Meeting on Food Traceability Rule Implementation Challenges** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA held a virtual public meeting on Nov. 6, 2026, the second in a quarterly series, to gather industry input on lot-level tracking, distribution-center record-keeping, and other implementation challenges under the Food Traceability Rule (FSMA Section 204). Written comments are due Dec. 6, 2026 in Docket No. FDA-2014-N-0053. *Why it matters:* Certain soft and semi-soft cheeses are on FDA's Food Traceability List, so how FDA resolves lot-level tracking flexibilities will shape future compliance costs for dairy processors; enforcement itself remains delayed until July 2028. [FDA.gov](https://www.fda.gov/food/workshops-meetings-webinars-food-and-dietary-supplements/fda-public-meeting-challenges-and-solutions-lot-level-food-traceability-11062026), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/10/fda-meeting-seeks-to-gather-input-on-exemptions-from-food-traceability-rule/)

### Nutrition
**USDA Adds $27M in Farm to School Grants and $50M in Cafeteria Equipment Funding** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA announced on Nov. 2, 2026 a new round of about $27 million in Patrick Leahy Farm to School Grants plus $50 million in National School Lunch Program equipment-assistance grants to state agencies, building on the August "Harvest to Hallways" initiative; the Farm to School program has now funded more than 149 projects nationwide in 2026. *Why it matters:* School meal programs are a significant channel for fluid milk and dairy consumption, so continued federal investment in local sourcing and cafeteria infrastructure is a modestly favorable backdrop, though the funding itself is not dairy-specific. [Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/policy/usda-announces-additional-2026-school-nutrition-and-equipment-grants)
