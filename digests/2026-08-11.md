**Federal Court Upholds EPA Exemption Shielding Farms From Air-Emissions Reporting** (Low · Monitor Only) — The U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia ruled Aug. 7 to uphold a 2019 EPA rule that exempts air emissions from animal waste at farms — including dairy operations — from reporting requirements under EPCRA. NMPF, which has litigated the issue for years, called it a near-resolution of a decades-long fight; environmental groups have pursued a related appeal. *Why it matters:* Affirms the status quo sparing dairy farms and their processor customers from federal air-emissions reporting; a reversal on appeal would impose new reporting costs and disclosure exposure across the dairy supply chain. [NMPF](https://www.nmpf.org/dairy-farmers-near-win-in-decades-long-regulatory-fight/), [Oklahoma Farm Report](https://www.oklahomafarmreport.com/2026/08/10/dairy-defined-podcast-dairy-farmers-near-win-in-decades-long-regulatory-fight/)
