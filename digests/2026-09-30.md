### Regulatory

**FDA Reminds Food Facilities to Renew Biennial Registration by Dec. 31** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA is reminding domestic and foreign facilities that manufacture, process, pack or hold food for human or animal consumption in the U.S. to renew their food facility registration during the Oct. 1–Dec. 31, 2026 biennial window required under FSMA. Qualified facilities must also submit a supporting attestation in the same window; renewal is free and filed electronically, and registrations not renewed by Dec. 31 are considered expired. *Why it matters:* A routine but mandatory compliance deadline for every domestic dairy manufacturing facility; an expired registration can bar a facility from legally shipping food in interstate commerce until it is restored. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-reminds-food-facilities-biennial-renewal-requirements), [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/regulatory-updates/2026/09/30/fda-reminds-food-facilities-to-renew-their-registration-by-dec-31/)

### Nutrition

**USDA's $70M School-Kitchen Equipment Grants Draw Criticism as Too Small for the 'Real Food' Push** (Low · Monitor Only) — HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s push for schools to serve more "real food" and scratch-cooked meals, and less ultra-processed food, is backed by $70 million in USDA federal grants for kitchen-equipment upgrades. School nutrition directors say the funding falls well short of what districts need for ovens, refrigeration, added kitchen space and staff training. *Why it matters:* Shows how the administration's "real food" framing is translating into a concrete but likely under-resourced school-meal procurement program, running alongside the separate HHS DEPEND pilot-funding awards already on the watch list; school-channel dairy formats (milk, cheese, yogurt) are directly affected by how much scratch-cooking infrastructure districts can actually build out. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/politics/trump-school-lunch-rfk-jr), [WSAW](https://wsaw.com/2026/09/29/trump-administration-is-pushing-real-food-school-here-are-obstacles-many-districts-face)

### Sustainability

**California Governor Vetoes AB 2253, the 'Protecting Consumers Against Greenwashing Act'** (Low · Monitor Only) — Gov. Gavin Newsom vetoed AB 2253, which would have tightened recycled-content marketing-claim requirements for packaging and eventually required conformance with the FTC's Green Guides. Newsom cited economic headwinds from federal tariff and trade policy, saying new compliance documentation requirements would burden manufacturers with little consumer benefit; AMERIPEN and the American Beverage Association opposed the bill, while Californians Against Waste and other environmental groups backed it. *Why it matters:* A modest compliance relief for packaged-food manufacturers, including dairy processors using plastic tubs, cartons and cups — it does not affect the state's broader SB 54 EPR packaging law, whose producer obligations still phase in starting January 2027. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/california-governor-gavin-newsom-vetoes-ab2253-recycled-content/831481/), [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/28/newsom-nixes-recycled-content-claims-bill/)

**WWF and SC Johnson Release Economic Case for a Federal Packaging EPR Framework** (Low · Monitor Only) — World Wildlife Fund and SC Johnson published an RTI-conducted economic analysis comparing a single federal extended producer responsibility (EPR) packaging framework against the current seven-state patchwork (CA, CO, MD, ME, MN, OR, WA). The analysis estimates a national framework could cut local governments' annual waste-management costs by 49–53%, generate $9.5 billion in state/local tax revenue over 10 years, and roughly halve producer compliance costs versus complying with up to 50 separate state frameworks. *Why it matters:* Adds a business-backed economic argument to the push for a federal EPR standard that would directly set compliance costs and design requirements for a national dairy manufacturer's packaging across every state it ships into, rather than the currently fragmented state-by-state approach already generating multiple watch-list items. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/federal-epr-packaging-push-world-wildlife-fund-sc-johnson/831753/)
