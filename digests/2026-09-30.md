### Sustainability

**California Governor Vetoes AB 2253, the 'Protecting Consumers Against Greenwashing Act'** (Low · Monitor Only) — Gov. Gavin Newsom vetoed AB 2253, which would have tightened recycled-content marketing-claim requirements for packaging and eventually required conformance with the FTC's Green Guides. Newsom cited economic headwinds from federal tariff and trade policy, saying new compliance documentation requirements would burden manufacturers with little consumer benefit; AMERIPEN and the American Beverage Association opposed the bill, while Californians Against Waste and other environmental groups backed it. *Why it matters:* A modest compliance relief for packaged-food manufacturers, including dairy processors using plastic tubs, cartons and cups — it does not affect the state's broader SB 54 EPR packaging law, whose producer obligations still phase in starting January 2027. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/california-governor-gavin-newsom-vetoes-ab2253-recycled-content/831481/), [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/28/newsom-nixes-recycled-content-claims-bill/)
