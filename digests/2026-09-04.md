**House Subcommittee Advances Bipartisan Bills to Federalize Packaging 'Recyclable' and 'Recycled Content' Claims** (Medium · Monitor Only) — On Sept. 1, 2026, the House Energy and Commerce Committee's Commerce, Manufacturing and Trade Subcommittee voice-voted to advance the PACK Act (H.R. 6832) and the Recycled Materials Attribution Act (H.R. 7502) to the full committee. The bills would set federal, third-party-certified standards for "recyclable," "compostable," "reusable" and "recycled content" packaging claims and would preempt the current patchwork of state rules; RMAA would also formally recognize mass-balance accounting for recycled-content claims. *Why it matters:* A single federal packaging-claims standard would ease compliance for a national dairy manufacturer navigating inconsistent state EPR and labeling laws, but the mass-balance provision is already drawing greenwashing criticism from environmental advocates.
