**Democratic Lawmakers Introduce Bill to Repeal Section 338 Tariffs, Including on Canadian Dairy** (Low · Monitor Only) — Sens. Kirsten Gillibrand (D-NY) and Peter Welch (D-VT), with Rep. Brad Schneider (D-IL) in the House, announced the bicameral "Banning Antiquated Duties and Delivering Equitable American Levies" (BAD DEAL) Act on Aug. 27, which would repeal all tariffs imposed under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 — including the 50% duties on Canadian dairy ingredients, alcohol and autos — and refund duties already collected. The sponsors plan to formally introduce the bill when the Senate returns in September. *Why it matters:* A legislative push to unwind the Section 338 tariffs runs directly alongside the US-Canada dairy trade dispute already on the watch list; passage odds are low in a Republican Congress, but it keeps tariff costs in the news ahead of Canada's Sept. 8 retaliatory tariffs.
