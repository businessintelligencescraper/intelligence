**AAP Renews Call to Ban Raw Milk Sales, Citing New H5N1 Risk** (Medium · Potential Media Interest) — The American Academy of Pediatrics published an updated clinical report, in the November 2026 issue of Pediatrics, renewing its longstanding call for a ban on the sale and distribution of raw milk and raw-milk dairy products. The statement cites established risks from Listeria, E. coli, Salmonella, Campylobacter and Brucella — noting nearly half of raw-dairy-linked illnesses occur in people under 20 — and adds a new concern: emerging evidence that H5N1 avian influenza can reach high concentrations in raw milk and remain detectable in refrigerated raw milk for up to five weeks.
*Why it matters:* Raw milk is a small, mostly state-regulated niche outside mainstream pasteurized dairy, but renewed advocacy from a major pediatric body — amplified by Washington Post coverage and the novel H5N1 angle — keeps "dairy and illness" headlines circulating and could prompt fresh state legislative attention to raw-milk sales rules. Pasteurized dairy manufacturers are not implicated, but category-level reputation and consumer-confidence spillover is possible if coverage grows.
