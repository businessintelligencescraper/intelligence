## New developments

### Regulatory
**Free-Market and Consumer-Rights Groups Urge OMB to Block FDA's Ultra-Processed Food Definition** (Low · Monitor Only) — Sixteen free-market and consumer-rights organizations sent a letter to White House budget director Russell Vought urging him to block the pending federal definition of "ultra-processed food," first reported by Axios on Aug. 27. The groups argue the classification lacks scientific grounding, would raise grocery costs through reformulation and repackaging, and would expose manufacturers to added litigation risk. *Why it matters:* This adds a new, ideologically distinct source of pressure — beyond the food-company CEOs and Consumer Brands Association pushback reported earlier this month — against the same pending-review UPF definition the company is already tracking, one HHS Secretary Kennedy has said could underpin a future front-of-pack labeling system. It signals the release timeline and final scope remain contested, not that anything has changed yet. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/27/free-market-groups-processed-food-definition)

### Nutrition
**HHS Launches $32.5 Million DEPEND Initiative to Fund School Nutrition Pilot Programs** (Low · Monitor Only) — HHS announced a $32.5 million initiative called DEPEND (Developing Evidence for Programs to Ensure Nutrition for Development) on Aug. 26 in Tampa, FL, led by the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality and NIH. It will fund school and school-district pilot programs testing ways to replace processed foods with nutrient-dense options and build an evidence base for what works; the application cycle opens soon, with first awards expected in October. *Why it matters:* This is a second MAHA-aligned school-nutrition push in three days, following the Aug. 24 Harvest to Hallways announcement, and continues the administration's framing around reducing "highly processed" foods in schools. Dairy is generally positioned as a "real food" category in this messaging, but the pilot criteria selected this fall could eventually shape school procurement standards the company sells into. [HHS.gov](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-depend-initiative-healthier-school-meals-real-food.html), [Health.gov](https://health.gov/news/hhs-announces-325-million-depend-initiative-transform-school-nutrition)
