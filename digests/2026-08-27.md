### Nutrition
**HHS Launches $32.5 Million DEPEND Initiative to Fund School Nutrition Pilot Programs** (Low · Monitor Only) — HHS announced a $32.5 million initiative called DEPEND (Developing Evidence for Programs to Ensure Nutrition for Development) on Aug. 26 in Tampa, FL, led by the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality and NIH. It will fund school and school-district pilot programs testing ways to replace processed foods with nutrient-dense options and build an evidence base for what works; the application cycle opens soon, with first awards expected in October. *Why it matters:* This is a second MAHA-aligned school-nutrition push in three days, following the Aug. 24 Harvest to Hallways announcement, and continues the administration's framing around reducing "highly processed" foods in schools. Dairy is generally positioned as a "real food" category in this messaging, but the pilot criteria selected this fall could eventually shape school procurement standards the company sells into.
