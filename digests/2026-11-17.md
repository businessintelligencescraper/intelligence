**State Animal-Health Officials Press USDA to Authorize Emergency H5N1 Vaccine Pilot for Dairy Cattle** (Medium · Potential Media Interest) — The National Association of State Departments of Agriculture (NASDA) and the National Assembly of State Animal Health Officials met with USDA and sent a letter Nov. 6 asking the department to authorize a voluntary, national emergency-use vaccination pilot for H5N1 in dairy cattle in willing states. The request comes as the outbreak has reached at least 22 states — most recently Indiana (Nov. 2) and Oregon (Nov. 10) — with nearly 1,200 dairy herds nationally testing positive since March 2024; no H5N1 vaccine is yet approved for U.S. dairy cattle, though several candidates remain in USDA field trials.
*Why it matters:* A formal, coordinated request from state animal-health regulators — not just industry groups — raises the odds USDA moves on emergency vaccine authorization; any dairy-cattle vaccination program would carry trade and export-market-access implications alongside its biosecurity benefit.
*Recommended action:* Monitor for a USDA response to the vaccine-authorization request; no action needed at this stage.
