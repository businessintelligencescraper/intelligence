### Dairy Industry

**U.S. Dairy Processors Plan Over $15 Billion in New Processing Capacity Across 23 States** (Low · Monitor Only) — IDFA's updated investment tracker, released during National Manufacturing Month, counts more than 85 dairy-processing capacity projects planned or underway through 2030, worth over $15 billion across 23 states — up from the roughly $13 billion/19-state figure IDFA cited in late August. New York leads at $2.8 billion, followed by Texas ($1.7B), Wisconsin ($1.4B), Idaho ($720M) and Iowa ($701M); by product, milk and cream account for the largest share ($5.63B), followed by cheese ($3.76B).
*Why it matters:* A fresh, higher investment figure underscores continued industry-wide confidence and capacity expansion even as tariff, EPR-litigation and regulatory pressures covered elsewhere on the watch list continue; useful context for growth and jobs messaging, and a data point comms teams may want on hand if asked about the industry's outlook.
