### Dairy Industry

**U.S. Dairy Processors Commit $13 Billion to Processing Capacity Expansion** (Low · Monitor Only) — Trade-press reporting details a $13 billion wave of new and expanded dairy-processing capacity underway across 19 states — the largest investment cycle the industry has seen in decades — aimed at handling a projected 15-billion-pound increase in U.S. milk production by 2030. New York, Texas, Michigan, Wisconsin and Idaho are the leading investment destinations; IDFA's president and CEO cited industry confidence in long-term demand, including rising demand for dairy protein.
*Why it matters:* Signals sustained, industry-wide confidence in category growth and expanding domestic processing capacity, reinforcing a positive supply/cost narrative and aligning the category with the broader high-protein consumer trend already reshaping grocery baskets. [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/13-billion-bet-why-dairy-processors-are-all-americas-protein-future)

**Dreyer's Voluntarily Recalls Select Outshine Fruit Bars Over Possible Glass Contamination** (Low · Monitor Only) — Dreyer's Grand Ice Cream, Inc. voluntarily recalled select flavors of its Outshine frozen fruit bars (Strawberry, Watermelon, Grape, Tangerine and Black Cherry 6-count boxes, plus a 24-count variety pack) nationwide on Aug. 18 after consumers reported finding glass fragments in the product. The company took the action out of an abundance of caution; no illnesses or injuries have been reported.
*Why it matters:* A voluntary, no-injury recall at a major frozen-dessert manufacturer is a reminder of how sensitive foreign-material contamination issues are for the broader dairy/frozen-treats category, even though the isolated cause, lack of illness reports, and prompt voluntary action limit any category-wide read-across.
