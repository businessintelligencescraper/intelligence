**Senate Companion Bill Introduced for Food Labeling Modernization Act, Adding Front-of-Pack 'High In' Warnings** (Medium · Potential Media Interest) — Sens. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT) and Cory Booker (D-NJ) introduced a Senate companion to the CSPI-backed Food Labeling Modernization Act of 2026, which would direct FDA to require front-of-package "High In" warning labels for saturated fat, sodium, and added sugar, add warnings on non-nutritive sweeteners, and define regulatory terms around "ultra-processed" marketing claims. The bill mirrors House companion H.R.8385 (Pallone/DeLauro, introduced April 20, 2026); trade press covered the Senate introduction on July 31.
*Why it matters:* This is a second, separate federal front-of-pack labeling vehicle now moving in the Senate alongside Sen. Sanders' already-tracked Childhood Diabetes Reduction Act, adding to legislative momentum toward mandatory saturated-fat/sugar warning labels that would directly affect whole milk, full-fat cheese, and sweetened dairy products. [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/policy-legislation/2026/07/31/two-democrats-introduce-food-labeling-modernization-act-in-senate/), [Civil Eats](https://civileats.com/2026/07/31/congress-moves-forward-on-two-food-labeling-bills/), [CSPI](https://www.cspi.org/press-release/senators-introduce-legislation-promote-transparent-food-labels-0)

**Lidl US Expands Allergen Recall of Imported Shortbread Cookies to Include Undeclared Milk** (Low · Monitor Only) — Lidl US expanded its recall of imported Eridanous-brand shortbread cookies (chocolate-truffle and apricot-filling varieties) to an additional SKU after finding that foreign-language labels failed to declare wheat, soy, milk, egg, and tree nut (coconut) allergens. The initial recall began July 24; the expansion was reported July 31. No illnesses have been reported; the product was distributed to Lidl stores in Delaware, D.C., Georgia, Maryland, and New Jersey.
*Why it matters:* Milk is one of the undeclared allergens driving this five-state recall, keeping allergen-labeling enforcement for imported goods in the news cycle even though the product itself is unrelated to the company's dairy portfolio; no direct company exposure identified. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/lidl-us-expands-recall-eridanous-shortbread-cookies-due-undeclared-wheat-soy-milk-egg-and-tree-nut)
