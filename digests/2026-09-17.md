**FDA Releases 2026 Food Code** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA published its 2026 Food Code on Sept. 17, 2026, the model food-safety regulations for retail and food service that state, local, tribal and territorial agencies may adopt. Changes from the 2022 edition include a new written employee-illness-policy requirement, a limited double-gloving exception, an added alternative cooling process for retail foods, and revised food-establishment and mobile-food-establishment definitions. *Why it matters:* The Food Code is the baseline retail/foodservice food-safety standard most states model their own rules on, so any facility-side or foodservice-adjacent operations will eventually see these provisions surface locally; there is nothing dairy-specific in this edition, making it a routine biennial update rather than a new compliance driver. [The Daily Intake](https://www.dailyintakeblog.com/2026/09/fda-publishes-2026-food-code/), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/retail-food-protection/fda-food-code)
