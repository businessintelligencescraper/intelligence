### Regulatory

**FDA Releases 2026 Food Code** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA published its 2026 Food Code on Sept. 17, 2026, the model food-safety regulations for retail and food service that state, local, tribal and territorial agencies may adopt. Changes from the 2022 edition include a new written employee-illness-policy requirement, a limited double-gloving exception, an added alternative cooling process for retail foods, and revised food-establishment and mobile-food-establishment definitions. *Why it matters:* The Food Code is the baseline retail/foodservice food-safety standard most states model their own rules on, so any facility-side or foodservice-adjacent operations will eventually see these provisions surface locally; there is nothing dairy-specific in this edition, making it a routine biennial update rather than a new compliance driver. [The Daily Intake](https://www.dailyintakeblog.com/2026/09/fda-publishes-2026-food-code/), [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/retail-food-protection/fda-food-code)

**FDA Executive Summary Reveals Clover Hill Dairy's Fatal Listeria Strain Persisted in Plant Since 2021** (Medium · Monitor Only) — FDA published an executive summary of its investigation into the Clover Hill Dairy soft-cheese Listeria outbreak (15 sickened, 1 death, closed in August), revealing that whole-genome sequencing matched the outbreak strain found in the plant's environment to patient samples dating back to 2021 — five years before the 2026 outbreak was identified. The summary, released alongside one on an unrelated Guatemalan-cantaloupe Salmonella outbreak, is part of a newer FDA practice of publishing post-investigation executive summaries for added transparency. *Why it matters:* The finding shows a resident Listeria strain went undetected in a dairy production environment for roughly five years despite routine sanitation and testing before it caused a fatal outbreak, reinforcing the case for rigorous environmental monitoring programs (including harborage-site and drain testing) at cheese and soft-dairy facilities; FDA may cite this case as it pushes for stronger environmental monitoring expectations industry-wide. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-listeria-monocytogenes-soft-cheese-june-2026), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/09/fda-releases-executive-summaries-on-two-outbreak-investigations/)
