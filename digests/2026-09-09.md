## ⚠ Priority items

**Industry-Funded Report on Infant Formula Botulism Outbreaks Draws Criticism Over Lack of Mandatory Rules** — The Reagan-Udall Foundation for the FDA published "Applying the Science of Infant Formula Safety," a report from a July 13 roundtable on Clostridium botulinum risk in powdered infant formula that was funded and co-prepared by ByHeart Inc. and the International Dairy Foods Association (IDFA). The report offers 22 recommendations on testing, data-sharing and supply-chain controls but sets no mandatory requirements; critics note no parent or patient advocate participated in the roundtable. *Why it matters:* IDFA, the dairy industry's primary trade association, funded and helped prepare the report, and the ingredient at the center of the underlying outbreaks (organic powdered whole milk supplied by Organic West Milk and processed by Dairy Farmers of America) sits squarely in the dairy supply chain. Renewed coverage framing this as industry policing itself carries reputational exposure for dairy trade groups and ingredient suppliers even where a given manufacturer is not implicated. *Recommended action:* Brief leadership on IDFA's role and prepare a short holding statement in case of press inquiries about industry-funded safety reviews; no proactive outreach needed.
