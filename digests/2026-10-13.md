### Dairy Industry

**USDA Imposes New 25% Cost-Share Mandate on Dairy Business Innovation Grant Program** (Low · Monitor Only) — The Wisconsin Cheese Makers Association publicly asked Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins to reverse a newly imposed 25% cost-share (matching-fund) requirement on USDA's four regional Dairy Business Innovation (DBI) Initiatives, which provide grants and technical assistance to dairy processors and farmers across 41 states and territories. WCMA's Oct. 6, 2026 release says the host organizations — public universities, a state agriculture department and a nonprofit trade association — lack unrestricted funds to cover the match, and that the mandate could halt grants and technical assistance the program has used to award more than $106 million to 911 dairy businesses since 2018.
*Why it matters:* DBI grants and technical assistance underpin smaller and mid-size dairy processors' product development and plant upgrades nationwide; a funding disruption would ripple through supply-chain partners and regional competitors rather than directly affect the company's own operations, but it is a fresh data point in a broader pattern of USDA tightening or restructuring dairy-sector support funding this year. [Wisconsin Cheese Makers Association (via Urban Milwaukee)](https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=1004400), [WisPolitics](https://www.wispolitics.com/?p=429023)

**APHIS Confirms First HPAI Cases in Washington Dairy Herds** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA's Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service confirmed highly pathogenic avian influenza (H5, clade 2.3.4.4b) in two dairy cattle herds in Washington on Oct. 8, 2026 — the state's first livestock detections of the virus. The herds had previously tested negative under the National Milk Testing Strategy and were tested after showing clinical signs; genetic sequencing to pin down the specific viral lineage is still pending.
*Why it matters:* Dairy cattle in 19 states have now been affected since the outbreak began in March 2024; APHIS says the detection does not change its eradication strategy and that the milk supply remains safe, but a new state joining the list keeps biosecurity and herd-sourcing monitoring relevant for any processor with supply ties to affected regions. [Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/dairy/hpai-confirmed-in-2-washington-dairy-herds), [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/new-hpai-cases-washington-put-dairy-farm-biosecurity-back-focus)
