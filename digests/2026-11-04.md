# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Daily Brief — November 4, 2026

## New developments

### Dairy Industry

**USDA Announces $472.5 Million Food Purchase, Including $165.5 Million in Dairy** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA announced on Oct. 29 a $472.5 million Section 32 purchase of American-grown commodities to support producers, with $165.5 million earmarked for dairy products alongside pork, chicken, rice, catfish and Pacific whiting. The purchase follows a $148 million round of dairy buys USDA announced in February and is intended to bolster demand during a soft stretch for milk prices.
*Why it matters:* Section 32 purchases are a routine but meaningful demand-support tool for the dairy sector; NMPF welcomed the announcement as help for producers amid weak milk prices, though it carries no direct exposure or action implication for a branded dairy manufacturer. [USDA](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/10/29/usda-announces-4725-million-food-purchase-support-us-producers-and-strengthen-americas-food-supply)

### Regulatory

**EPA Ends 20-Year-Old Agreement to Develop Livestock Air-Emissions Methodologies** (Low · Monitor Only) — EPA announced Oct. 30 that it will not finalize air-emissions estimating methodologies for animal feeding operations under the 2005 Air Compliance Agreement, citing 2005-2007 monitoring data as too old and limited to support reliable methods. EPA said it will notify the roughly 2,600 AFOs covered by the voluntary agreement and move to terminate it; the agency says the action does not change existing Clean Air Act permitting requirements.
*Why it matters:* NMPF welcomed the move, which closes out two decades of regulatory uncertainty for dairy farms tied to a voluntary monitoring program rather than imposing new obligations; it is a deregulatory, industry-favorable development with no near-term compliance burden for processors. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/newsreleases/trump-epa-brings-closure-20-year-old-agreement-ending-decades-long-uncertainty)

### Public Affairs

**IDFA Flags Dairy Export Barriers in Canada, China, EU and Others to USTR** (Low · Monitor Only) — IDFA submitted comments Oct. 29 to USTR for the 2027 National Trade Estimate Report, citing ongoing trade barriers to U.S. dairy exports in Canada, China, the EU, Indonesia, India, Malaysia, Mexico and Turkiye — markets representing over $5 billion, more than half of 2025 U.S. dairy exports. IDFA highlighted Canadian tariff-rate-quota administration and milk-class pricing, EU environmental reporting rules, and registration requirements in Turkiye and Malaysia as the most pressing issues.
*Why it matters:* Routine annual advocacy input into the federal trade-policy process; flags Canada's dairy TRQ administration and EU due-diligence/reporting rules as recurring friction points relevant to any dairy exporter, but carries no immediate business impact. [IDFA](https://www.idfa.org/news/idfa-urges-u-s-government-to-address-global-trade-barriers-and-expand-market-opportunities-for-u-s-dairy)
