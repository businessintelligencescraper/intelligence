**California Governor Signs First-in-Nation 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' Food Label Law** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required) — Gov. Gavin Newsom has signed AB 2244, creating a voluntary state certification program and a "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" seal for foods meeting California's non-UPF standard, which excludes products containing synthetic dyes, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and certain other processing aids. The California Department of Public Health will oversee the program, with third-party certifiers to be accredited by June 1, 2029, and large grocery stores required to give certified products distinct in-store display. *Why it matters:* This is the first state law to build a certification/labeling program around a legal ultra-processed-food definition, building on California's 2025 AB 1264; its qualifying criteria touch additives, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and processing aids common in flavored milk, cultured dairy and processed cheese, creating both a potential marketing opportunity for compliant products and a new labeling distinction other states may adopt as a template.
