### Dairy Industry
**Dairy Industry Completes Pledge to Remove Artificial Colors From School Milk, Cheese and Yogurt** (Low · Potential Media Interest) — America's dairy processors have fulfilled IDFA's voluntary "Healthy Dairy in Schools Commitment," eliminating seven certified artificial colors (Red 3, Red 40, Green 3, Blue 1, Blue 2, Yellow 5, Yellow 6) from milk, cheese and yogurt sold into the National School Lunch and School Breakfast Programs, ahead of the 2026-2027 school year. It is the second consecutive year the industry has delivered on a voluntary school-nutrition pledge, following last year's reduction of added sugar in flavored school milk.
*Why it matters:* A positive, verifiable industry-wide compliance story that surpasses state and federal standards and gives dairy manufacturers a concrete talking point amid ongoing public and legislative scrutiny of artificial food dyes; likely to draw trade-press and possibly general-media pickup given the national debate over food dyes in schools. ### Sustainability
**Bipartisan Senate Bill Would Fund Dairy Manure-Management Upgrades** (Low · Monitor Only) — Sen. Alex Padilla (D-CA) reintroduced the Converting Our Waste Sustainably (COWS) Act, with Sen. Martin Heinrich (D-NM) signing on as a cosponsor in mid-August. The bill would create a voluntary USDA grant program, built on the existing Environmental Quality Incentives Program, to help dairy and other livestock producers install alternative manure-management systems that cut methane emissions and improve water and air quality.
*Why it matters:* Manure management and methane reduction are recurring themes in dairy sustainability reporting and farmer-supplier engagement; a funded voluntary federal program would give the company's farmer-suppliers a cost-effective emissions-reduction pathway without new mandates. The bill has not been scheduled for committee markup and is one of several farm-sustainability measures competing for floor time this Congress, so near-term impact is limited. [Sen. Martin Heinrich (Senate.gov)](https://www.heinrich.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/heinrich-cosponsors-cows-act-to-reduce-greenhouse-emissions-support-sustainable-waste-practices), [New Mexico Political Report](https://nmpoliticalreport.com/2026/08/23/money-for-manure-heinrich-joins-congressional-effort-to-pay-farmers-to-manage-mountains-of-manure/)
