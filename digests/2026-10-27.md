**90+ Farm, Labor and Consumer Groups Urge Congress to Advance Dairy Market Stabilization Bill** (Medium · Monitor Only) — A coalition of more than 90 farmer, rural, labor, processor, consumer and environmental organizations, led by the National Family Farm Coalition, sent a letter to Congress on Oct. 23 urging action on the Milk From Family Dairies Act of 2026 (S.4906), which has sat in the Senate Agriculture Committee without a hearing since Sen. Welch introduced it (with Sen. Sanders co-sponsoring) in June 2026.
*Why it matters:* The bill would create a dairy market stabilization program with size-scaled price floors and production quotas set by regional and national boards, funded by fees on over-quota milk — a supply-management structure that would directly reshape how processors source and pay for raw milk if it ever advances. The bill remains stalled without a scheduled hearing, but the breadth of the coalition signals building political pressure worth tracking alongside Farm Bill negotiations.
