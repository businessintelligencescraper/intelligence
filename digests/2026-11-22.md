**FDA and CDC Declare Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw-Milk Cheese E. coli Outbreak Over** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA and CDC closed out the multistate E. coli O26:H11 outbreak linked to Sierra Nevada Graziers raw-milk cheese, first identified in September 2026. The final tally stands at 18 illnesses across 10 states; the outbreak strain was matched to a sample of Graziers raw medium cheddar collected by California public health officials.
