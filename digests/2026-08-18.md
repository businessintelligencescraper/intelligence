# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Daily Brief — August 18, 2026

## ⚠ Priority items

**Canada Rejects Latest US Tariff Offer Hours Before Aug. 19 Dairy Tariff Deadline** — With roughly 12 hours left before 50% Section 338 tariffs on Canadian-origin dairy ingredients (whey, milk protein concentrate, lactose, casein/caseinates, milk powders) are set to take effect, Canadian officials told reporters they are not satisfied with a new US proposal offered Tuesday, Aug. 18, which would have only partially reduced sectoral tariffs (e.g., halving auto tariffs to 12.5%). Negotiators remain "far apart," according to sources, with no deal reached as of Tuesday evening.
*Why it matters:* This is the last realistic negotiating window before the tariffs take effect just after midnight Wednesday; without a deal, US processors sourcing Canadian-origin whey, milk protein concentrate, lactose or milk powder face an immediate cost increase, and dairy-quota terms remain unresolved.
*Recommended action:* Continue monitoring overnight for a last-minute deal announcement or confirmation the tariffs take effect as scheduled; no change to existing guidance.
[CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/canada-unhappy-u-s-offer-tariffs-9.7304923), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/08/18/tariffs-trump-canada-usmca-trade/975bbd3a-9ab9-11f1-9cc4-2dc9b46e2d5c_story.html)

**Carney and Trump Speak Directly by Phone Hours Before Tariff Deadline** — Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and US President Trump spoke by phone Monday afternoon, Aug. 17, about "the ongoing trade negotiations," the Prime Minister's Office confirmed, without disclosing further detail or any outcome. Carney separately described the negotiations as "very intense and delicate," with the tariffs still set to take effect just after midnight Wednesday, Aug. 19.
*Why it matters:* A direct leader-to-leader call is the most senior level of engagement yet in this round of talks, days after trade-minister and cabinet-level meetings produced no deal; it signals both governments are still working a resolution in the final hours, but no outcome was confirmed, so cost exposure on Canadian-origin dairy ingredient inputs remains live heading into the deadline.
*Recommended action:* Continue monitoring overnight for a deal announcement or confirmation the tariffs take effect as scheduled; no change to existing guidance.
