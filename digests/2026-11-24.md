**Bird Flu Reaches a Third Washington County, Pushing State's Dairy-Herd Count to 44** (Low · Monitor Only) — The Washington State Department of Agriculture confirmed H5N1 avian influenza in a Grant County dairy herd on Nov. 23, 2026, making Grant the third Washington county (after Yakima and Benton) with an infected herd and bringing the state's cumulative total to 44 affected herds since the outbreak began in early October. Officials have not publicly identified the farm; it is under quarantine, and no human cases have been linked to this herd. *Why it matters:* The Washington dairy-herd outbreak — already genetically traced to cattle movement from Idaho — keeps expanding in both scale and geography even as officials maintain the public-health risk is low and pasteurized milk remains safe; continued spread keeps biosecurity and worker-safety questions live for any processor sourcing from or operating in the Pacific Northwest dairy region.
