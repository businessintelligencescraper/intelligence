**FDA Assigns Highest-Risk Classification to Fatal Clover Hill Dairy Cheese Listeria Recall** — The FDA on Wednesday, August 5 assigned a Class I risk classification — its highest — to the recall of Clover Hill Dairy's cheese products, made at the company's Mechanicsville, Maryland facility. The recall, which began in June 2026 with the company's soft ricotta/requeson line and was expanded to all of its cheese products after Listeria monocytogenes contamination was confirmed, has been linked to 9 illnesses, 8 hospitalizations, and 1 death across Maryland, New York, and Virginia; over 29,200 lbs of cheese were distributed in bulk and at retail, including under relabeled brand names such as Kesso, Quesos La Ricura, Izalco, De Mi Pueblo, and Rio Lindo. *Why it matters:* A Class I classification — FDA's determination that a product carries a reasonable probability of causing serious illness or death — on a fatal Listeria outbreak traced to a dairy cheese manufacturer is a direct, live case study in the category-wide reputational and regulatory exposure a soft-cheese contamination event can carry; it's a prompt to review environmental-monitoring and Listeria-control programs at any facility producing soft/fresh cheeses, particularly given FDA traced the outbreak strain back to illness onset as early as 2023. *Recommended action:* Brief food-safety/quality leadership on the Class I classification; use as a prompt to review environmental-monitoring and Listeria-control protocols for any soft-cheese production lines; monitor for further CDC/FDA outbreak updates. ### Food Industry

**Salmonella Outbreak Tied to Mexican Jalapeños Served at Chipotle and Qdoba Grows to 345 Illnesses in 27 States** (Medium · Monitor Only) — CDC and FDA reported a multistate Salmonella Javiana outbreak linked to jalapeño peppers from a Sinaloa, Mexico grower, distributed by Coast Citrus Distributors, has reached 345 confirmed illnesses and 36 hospitalizations across 27 states, with illness onset dates between June 19 and July 20. The peppers were served at Chipotle and Qdoba locations; Coast Citrus has recalled remaining product and both chains have pulled jalapeños from affected restaurants. *Why it matters:* Not a dairy product and outside the company's own supply chain, but a 27-state, 345-illness outbreak tied to two major national restaurant chains is one of the larger multistate foodborne-illness events of the year and a useful live benchmark for recall scope, CDC/FDA coordination, and QSR-partner fallout — the same dynamics the company would face if a dairy ingredient were implicated in a comparable outbreak.
