**Senate Agriculture Committee Farm Bill Markup Fails on Party-Line Vote Over SNAP Dispute** (Low · Monitor Only) — The Senate Agriculture Committee voted 10-11 against advancing the Agricultural Act of 2026 ("Farm Bill 2.0") on August 6, after Republicans and Democrats deadlocked over how long a delay states should get before facing SNAP cost-sharing tied to payment-error rates. The only amendment to win approval was Sen. Thune's language reinstating mandatory country-of-origin labeling for beef; Chairman Boozman said the committee will try again after the Senate returns from its late-summer recess in September. *Why it matters:* The bill's dairy title — mandatory manufacturer cost/yield reporting tied to Federal Milk Marketing Order pricing, plus reauthorization of the Dairy Indemnity Payment Program and Dairy Promotion and Research Board through 2031 — was not itself the sticking point (SNAP was), but the whole bill is now stalled until at least September, delaying both the new reporting mandate and the program reauthorizations. ### Food Industry

**Salmonella Outbreak Tied to Mexican Jalapeños Served at Chipotle and Qdoba Grows to 345 Illnesses in 27 States** (Medium · Monitor Only) — CDC and FDA reported a multistate Salmonella Javiana outbreak linked to jalapeño peppers from a Sinaloa, Mexico grower, distributed by Coast Citrus Distributors, has reached 345 confirmed illnesses and 36 hospitalizations across 27 states, with illness onset dates between June 19 and July 20. The peppers were served at Chipotle and Qdoba locations; Coast Citrus has recalled remaining product and both chains have pulled jalapeños from affected restaurants. *Why it matters:* Not a dairy product and outside the company's own supply chain, but a 27-state, 345-illness outbreak tied to two major national restaurant chains is one of the larger multistate foodborne-illness events of the year and a useful live benchmark for recall scope, CDC/FDA coordination, and QSR-partner fallout — the same dynamics the company would face if a dairy ingredient were implicated in a comparable outbreak. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/salmonella/outbreaks/javiana-08-26/index.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/salmonella-outbreak-linked-to-mexican-jalapenos-sickens-345-across-27-us-states.html), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/qdoba-jalapenos-salmonella-outbreak-affects-dozens-of-states/)
