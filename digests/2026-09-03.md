### Legislative
**California Legislature Passes First-of-Its-Kind 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' Food Label Bill** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required) — California's Assembly Bill 2244 cleared the Legislature on Aug. 31 with unanimous votes (32-0 in the Senate, 72-0 in the Assembly) and was sent to Gov. Gavin Newsom on Sept. 1. The bill creates a voluntary state certification seal, "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified," for products meeting standards that exclude synthetic dyes, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and certain processing aids; Newsom has until Sept. 30, 2026 to sign or veto it and is reportedly expected to sign. *Why it matters:* This would be the first state-level certification program built around a UPF definition, giving California a template other states may copy; because the qualifying criteria touch additives, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and processing aids common in flavored milk, cultured dairy and processed cheese, it creates both a potential marketing opportunity for compliant products and a competitive/labeling distinction for products that would not qualify.
