**Consumer and Environmental Groups Rally Against FRESH Act Food-Safety Preemption Bill** (Low · Monitor Only) — The Environmental Working Group held a Capitol Hill rally opposing the FRESH Act (H.R. 8578), which would make FDA the sole regulator of food chemicals and additives and preempt state food-safety laws such as PFAS-in-packaging bans, artificial-dye restrictions in school food, and infant-formula heavy-metal disclosure requirements. Several members of Congress joined the rally opposing the bill, which remains in the House Agriculture Committee with no scheduled markup since its April referral. *Why it matters:* The bill's preemption clause would directly override state laws already on this watch list, including New York's pending GRAS-disclosure and infant-formula heavy-metal-testing bills. ### Dairy Industry

**Dairy Farmers of America Withdraws From IDFA Over Milk-Pricing Reform Petition** (Medium · Monitor Only) — Dairy Farmers of America (DFA), the nation's largest dairy cooperative with 83 processing facilities, announced it is ending its membership in the International Dairy Foods Association (IDFA) after IDFA proceeded with a USDA petition to change how "make allowances" (estimated processing costs) are set in the Federal Milk Marketing Order system without full membership consensus. DFA said the move put it in the untenable position of appearing to endorse a milk-pricing policy position it believes conflicts with its farmer-owners' interests; IDFA had pursued the single-issue petition ahead of the National Milk Producers Federation, which is separately asking USDA to consider more comprehensive FMMO pricing changes. *Why it matters:* This is a rare, public fracture between the dairy industry's two largest trade associations over federal milk-pricing reform, a process that directly affects processor input costs; it signals the FMMO make-allowance fight is deepening rather than resolving. [Agri-Pulse](https://www.agri-pulse.com/articles/19576-dairy-farmers-of-america-withdraws-from-idfa-over-fmmo-proposal), [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/policy/largest-dairy-cooperative-withdraws-international-dairy-foods-association)
