## New developments

### Dairy Industry

**Dairy Farmers of America Withdraws From IDFA Over Milk-Pricing Reform Petition** (Medium · Monitor Only) — Dairy Farmers of America (DFA), the nation's largest dairy cooperative with 83 processing facilities, announced it is ending its membership in the International Dairy Foods Association (IDFA) after IDFA proceeded with a USDA petition to change how "make allowances" (estimated processing costs) are set in the Federal Milk Marketing Order system without full membership consensus. DFA said the move put it in the untenable position of appearing to endorse a milk-pricing policy position it believes conflicts with its farmer-owners' interests; IDFA had pursued the single-issue petition ahead of the National Milk Producers Federation, which is separately asking USDA to consider more comprehensive FMMO pricing changes. *Why it matters:* This is a rare, public fracture between the dairy industry's two largest trade associations over federal milk-pricing reform, a process that directly affects processor input costs; it signals the FMMO make-allowance fight is deepening rather than resolving, and comes as IDFA and NMPF have otherwise presented a unified front on other issues like this year's ultra-processed-food warning-label fight (S. 5026).
