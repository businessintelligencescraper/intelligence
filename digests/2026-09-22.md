### Sustainability

**UK Packaging-Fee Data Shows Flexible Plastic Packaging Facing Sharply Higher Recycling Costs** (Low · Monitor Only) — New recyclability assessment data published under the UK's extended producer responsibility (pEPR) packaging scheme found that nearly two-thirds of flexible plastic packaging assessed in 2025 received the lowest recyclability rating, which will carry materially higher waste-disposal fees than better-rated packaging once fee modulation begins. Industry sources cited in the coverage estimated it could cost roughly $40 million to convert a single mid-size packaging line to a higher-rated format. *Why it matters:* Flexible pouches and films are common dairy ingredient and consumer packaging formats; while this fee structure is UK-specific, it is a concrete, dollars-and-cents example of how recyclability-based fee modulation can raise packaging costs once it takes effect — the same design being debated in US state EPR programs (California SB 54, Oregon, Colorado) that already sit on the watch list.
