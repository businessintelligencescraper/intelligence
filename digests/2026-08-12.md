### Legislative

**Bicameral Bill Would Set Federal Standards for "Recyclable" and "Compostable" Package Labeling** (Low · Monitor Only) — Sen. Jeff Merkley (D-OR) and Rep. Lou Correa (D-CA) introduced the TRUTH in Labeling Act of 2026, which would direct EPA and the FTC to jointly set enforceable federal definitions and a standardized on-pack label for "recyclable," "compostable," "reusable," and "refillable" claims, with tiered thresholds (e.g., a lower ~40% local-collection rate triggering a "qualified" recyclable claim). The bicameral bill is backed by recycling and composting trade groups including the Recycled Materials Association and The Recycling Partnership.
*Why it matters:* Food and beverage packaging routinely carries these claims, so a single federal standard would replace the fast-growing state-by-state patchwork of EPR and labeling rules (California SB 54, Oregon, Colorado, Maine, Maryland, Minnesota, Washington) the company already tracks; worth watching as a potential preemption vehicle, though this is an early-stage introduction with no committee action scheduled yet. Jeff Merkley (official release)](https://www.merkley.senate.gov/merkley-correa-launch-new-effort-to-fix-misleading-recycling-labels/), [Packaging Dive](https://www.packagingdive.com/news/truth-in-labeling-act-correa-merkley/827421/), [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/08/10/bicameral-truth-in-labeling-act-introduced-in-congress/)

### Nutrition

**GLP-1 Weight-Loss Drug Use Continues to Reshape Grocery Spending, New Data Shows** (Low · Monitor Only) — Numerator's latest quarterly tracker found 22% of US households now include a GLP-1 medication user, roughly double the share in 2023, and that GLP-1 households' overall grocery spending is down about 4% as they shift purchases toward protein, fiber and other functional-nutrition items and away from pantry/carb staples.
*Why it matters:* A mainstream, GLP-1-driven shift toward high-protein, nutrient-dense eating is a demand tailwind worth tracking for high-protein dairy categories (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, protein milk) even as it pressures overall grocery-basket spending among affected households. [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/glp1-weight-loss-drugs-medication-grocery-spend-numerator/827405/)

### Dairy Industry

**Bongards' Creameries Announces $135 Million Process Cheese Facility Expansion** (Low · Monitor Only) — Bongards' Creameries, a Minnesota dairy cooperative, announced Aug. 11, 2026 plans to invest $135 million to expand its process cheese facility in Bongards, MN — one of the largest capital investments in the co-op's 118-year history. The project adds roughly 120,000 sq. ft. of production space and 180 million lbs/year of process cheese capacity; construction begins Q4 2026 with commercial operations expected in early 2028.
*Why it matters:* A capital investment of this scale signals continued confidence in US process-cheese demand and rising category production capacity; useful competitive/category context for the company's own capacity and investment planning, with no regulatory or reputational dimension. [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/bongards-bets-big-135-million-expansion-signals-confidence-process-cheese-future), [Refrigerated & Frozen Food](https://www.refrigeratedfrozenfood.com/articles/104635-bongards-creamiers-slates-135m-expansion-of-minnesota-cheese-facility)
