## ⚠ Priority items

**76 Dairy Companies and Co-ops Join NMPF, IDFA in Urging Congress to Reject Ultra-Processed Warning-Label Bill** — The National Milk Producers Federation and International Dairy Foods Association, joined by 76 dairy companies, cooperatives and state and regional producer organizations, sent congressional leaders a letter urging rejection of S. 5026, the Childhood Diabetes Reduction Act of 2026. The bill would create a broad "ultra-processed foods" definition and require front-of-package warning labels on foods it covers, including whole milk, cheese and most yogurts. NMPF President and CEO Gregg Doud said the bill "gets the science wrong, lumping wholesome dairy foods in with products that have nothing in common with them nutritionally." *Why it matters:* S. 5026 already awaits a Senate floor vote and would apply blanket UPF warning labels to nutrient-dense dairy staples; this coalition letter shows the scale of industry mobilization against it just ahead of that vote. *Recommended action:* Brief leadership on the coalition letter and confirm the company's own positioning relative to NMPF/IDFA messaging before any Senate floor vote on S. 5026.
