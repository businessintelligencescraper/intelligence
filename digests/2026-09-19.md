### Sustainability

**House Energy & Commerce Committee Advances Federal Packaging-Claims Bills to Full House** (Medium · Monitor Only) — The House Energy and Commerce Committee voted Sept. 16, 2026 to advance the PACK Act (H.R. 6832) and the Recycled Materials Attribution Act (H.R. 7502) out of full committee to the full House floor, as part of a bipartisan 16-bill markup. The bills would set federal, FTC-enforced standards for "recyclable," "compostable," "reusable," and "recycled content" packaging claims — including formal recognition of mass-balance accounting — preempting the current patchwork of state rules. *Why it matters:* This is the next procedural step for bills already on the radar (subcommittee advance covered Sept. 1): full-committee passage moves a potential federal packaging-claims standard closer to a House floor vote, which would matter for a national dairy manufacturer's packaging labeling compliance across inconsistent state EPR and claims laws. The mass-balance recycled-content provision continues to be the main point of contention, drawing greenwashing criticism from environmental advocates even as it advances with bipartisan support.
