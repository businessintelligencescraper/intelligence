**Multistate E. coli Outbreak Tied to Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw Milk Cheese Prompts Recall** — FDA and CDC are investigating a multistate E. coli O26:H11 outbreak linked to Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheese (medium and sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, Monterey jack). As of Sept. 25, 13 people across nine states have been sickened, 8 hospitalized, and 3 have developed hemolytic uremic syndrome (HUS); more than half of those sickened are children age 5 or younger. Illness onset dates run July 7–Aug. 26, 2026, and FDA contacted the firm Sept. 24 to request a recall. *Why it matters:* A severe, multistate outbreak with HUS cases concentrated in young children and national broadcast coverage is a live reminder of the category-wide reputational and regulatory scrutiny cheese-safety incidents draw, even though the implicated product is unpasteurized raw-milk cheese, outside a conventional pasteurized-dairy manufacturer's line. *Recommended action:* Brief food-safety/communications leadership on the outbreak; confirm no supply-chain or private-label link; monitor for further FDA/CDC updates. **Sweeping ICE Enforcement Operation in Southwest Kansas Disrupts Dairy and Meatpacking Workforce** — A multi-day Immigration and Customs Enforcement operation across Dodge City, Garden City and Liberal, Kansas that began around Sept. 23 has led to dozens of detentions at meatpacking plants and at least one large dairy; local officials say workers at a Liberal-area dairy have not left the premises since the operation began out of fear of being detained. National Beef Packing Co. and other processors report delayed cattle shipments and reduced slaughter volumes (down 14–16% week over week) as employees stay home. *Why it matters:* Dairy groups estimate that dairies employing immigrant labor produce close to 80% of the US milk supply, so a sustained, multi-day enforcement action of this scale in a major dairy and cattle region is a live test of workforce-continuity risk that any national dairy manufacturer's milk-supply footprint shares exposure to; it is also a fast-moving reputational and political flashpoint, with advocates reporting detentions of people with no criminal record. *Recommended action:* Brief supply-chain and government-affairs leads on potential milk-supply and labor exposure in Kansas and comparable sourcing regions; monitor for spread of enforcement activity elsewhere and for industry-group statements.
