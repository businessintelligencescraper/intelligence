# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Daily Brief — October 5, 2026

## New developments

**IDFA warns EU corporate due-diligence rules are raising costs for U.S. dairy exporters** (Low · Monitor Only) — The International Dairy Foods Association told U.S. trade officials that the EU's Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive and related supply-chain rules are imposing significant compliance costs on U.S. dairy processors and farmers, including some that don't sell directly into Europe. IDFA said members report initial social audits costing thousands of dollars per facility, with a full audit cycle plus follow-up approaching $27,000 per site and over 100 staff hours.
*Why it matters:* Even companies without direct EU sales can be pulled into compliance costs through supply-chain due-diligence requirements from EU-facing customers or co-packers, making this a cost and resourcing issue for the broader U.S. dairy sector, not just exporters. IDFA is pressing the administration to seek a U.S. exemption from the rule's extraterritorial reach, which is the lever worth watching.
