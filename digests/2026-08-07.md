## New developments

### Regulatory

**Federal Court Orders USDA to Rewrite Bioengineered Food Labeling Rule, Closing Refined-Ingredient Loophole** (Medium · Monitor Only) — A federal court ruled that USDA's exemption of highly refined bioengineered ingredients (such as refined corn oil and sugar) from mandatory GMO disclosure was arbitrary, and separately found that QR-code-only compliance is unlawful. USDA must rewrite the 2016 bioengineered food disclosure rule, with the challenged provisions void by January 1, 2028 if not replaced; USDA's own regulatory agenda reportedly targets a proposed rule as soon as August 2026.
*Why it matters:* Broadens mandatory bioengineered-ingredient disclosure to more processed foods, including dairy products that rely on refined sweeteners or oils, and disallows QR-code-only labeling, a compliance approach some manufacturers use; the rulemaking that follows is worth tracking for scope and timeline. [DTN/Progressive Farmer](https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/news/article/2026/08/05/federal-court-orders-usda-rewrite), [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/gmos-bioengineered-food-labeling-law-court-usda-rewrite-upfs/827249/)

**FDA Finalizes Updated Guidance on Assessing Antimicrobial Resistance Risk for New Animal Drugs** (Low · Monitor Only) — FDA's Center for Veterinary Medicine published a final revised Guidance for Industry #152 on August 6, updating the methodology used to evaluate whether a new antimicrobial drug proposed for food-producing animals could promote antibiotic-resistant bacteria that pose a risk to human health, including revised criteria for ranking drug classes' importance to human medicine.
*Why it matters:* Sets the approval bar for new antimicrobials used in food-producing animals, including dairy-cattle mastitis treatments; doesn't affect currently marketed drugs, but is a marker of continued regulatory scrutiny of antibiotic use in food animals. [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/06/2026-15951/evaluating-the-safety-of-antimicrobial-new-animal-drugs-with-regard-to-their-microbiological-effects)

### Legislative

**Federal Judge Orders Narrow Supplemental Briefing in Oregon Packaging-EPR Bellwether Trial** (Medium · Monitor Only) — Following a five-day bench trial in July testing the constitutionality of Oregon's producer-funded packaging EPR law, Judge Michael H. Simon decided oral argument was unnecessary and instead ordered supplemental briefing — due August 7, 2026 — on whether the law's exemption for certain small public-body producers is facially discriminatory under the Dormant Commerce Clause. A ruling is expected by the end of August.
*Why it matters:* This is the first full federal trial testing the constitutionality of a state packaging EPR program; industry counsel describe it as a bellwether whose outcome could reshape or invalidate elements of EPR programs across all seven states that have enacted them, directly affecting packaging compliance costs and design obligations for dairy manufacturers. [Bergeson & Campbell, P.C.](https://www.lawbc.com/federal-court-requests-supplemental-briefing-in-oregon-epr-challenge/), [NAW](https://www.naw.org/statement-on-oregon-epr-trial/)

### Nutrition

**New Study Finds No Cardiometabolic Harm From Three Daily Servings of Full-Fat Dairy** (Low · Potential Media Interest) — A 12-week University of Toronto study of 74 adults, published in the Journal of Nutrition, found that three daily servings of full-fat dairy caused no significant adverse changes to body weight, body fat, or cholesterol compared with a lower-dairy-fat diet, adding to evidence for the "dairy matrix" theory that dairy fat behaves differently than other saturated fats.
*Why it matters:* Provides fresh peer-reviewed support for full-fat dairy consistent with the 2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines' recommendation of whole/full-fat dairy options, and is useful evidence for nutrition-communications and product-positioning conversations. [DairyReporter](https://www.dairyreporter.com/Article/2026/08/05/study-backs-three-daily-servings-of-full-fat-dairy/)
