### Dairy Industry

**Sargento to Acquire Dips, Spreads and Quiche Maker La Terra Fina** (Low · Monitor Only) — Sargento Foods signed a definitive agreement to acquire California-based La Terra Fina, a category leader in dairy-based dips, spreads and quiches with roughly 250 employees and a 100,000-sq-ft facility near San Francisco. Terms were not disclosed; the deal is expected to close in late September 2026.
*Why it matters:* A major US cheese manufacturer expanding into adjacent dairy-based deli categories is a useful competitive-intelligence data point on where dairy-industry peers see growth, and a reminder that category consolidation continues even amid a soft cheese-pricing environment. [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/sargento-la-terra-fina-dips-spreads-quiches-cheese-acquisition/826824/), [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/industry-news-developments/2026/07/31/sargento-to-acquire-la-terra-fina-maker-of-dips-spreads-quiches/)

**USDA Commits $25M to Arizona Screwworm Facility as Mexican Cattle Port Reopening Nears** (Low · Monitor Only) — USDA Secretary Brooke Rollins announced a $25 million commitment to a new sterile-fly dispersal facility at the Douglas, Arizona port of entry, part of the phased reopening of cross-border cattle trade with Mexico that has been closed since May 2025 over New World screwworm. Douglas is set to reopen to livestock imports on August 24, with every animal undergoing full USDA inspection.
*Why it matters:* New World screwworm is a direct animal-health threat to cattle, including dairy cows and replacement heifers, and the border closure has constrained the North American cattle supply chain feeding the dairy herd; the phased reopening is an early, positive signal but remains contingent on no new detections near the border. [DTN/Progressive Farmer](https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/livestock/article/2026/08/04/rollins-announces-25m-arizona-nws), [Southeast AgNet](https://southeastagnet.com/2026/08/03/usda-invests-arizona-sterile-fly-facility-fight-screwworm/)

**Canadian Cheesemaker Recalls 33 White Cheddar Products Over Listeria Contamination** (Low · Monitor Only) — Laiterie de Coaticook recalled 33 White Cheddar cheese products — plain and flavored, in sizes from 50g to 2kg — after testing found possible Listeria monocytogenes contamination. The Canadian Food Inspection Agency issued the recall notice August 3 for product distributed in Quebec; no illnesses had been reported as of the notice.
*Why it matters:* A cheese-specific Listeria recall at a Canadian processor lands the same week a US Class I Listeria cheese recall (Clover Hill Dairy) is fresh in the news cycle — a reminder that soft/curd cheese Listeria risk is a persistent, cross-border category exposure, even though this recall has no link to the company's own supply chain. [CFIA](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/certain-coaticook-brand-cheddar-cheese-recalled-due-listeria-monocytogenes), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/08/33-cheeses-recalled-in-canada-because-of-listeria-concerns/)
