These are the feeds and topic monitors the system sweeps every weekday morning. Each one is checked for items published in the last 36 hours, then filtered for relevance and for whether the outlet meets the reputable-source bar.

Not everything here becomes a story. A source appearing on this list means it is watched — not that anything it publishes is reported. Items from unidentified blogs, content farms and unedited press-release wires are discarded even when they surface through one of these monitors.
