Two kinds of source are listed below. Named feeds watch one publisher directly — the FDA newsroom, the Federal Register, Food Dive. Search monitors watch a subject rather than a publisher, and will return that subject from any outlet that covers it.

This means national press is included without being listed by name. Reuters, CBS News, STAT News and Consumer Reports have all appeared in briefs through the topic monitors, despite none of them being a named source. The outlets that have actually come through are shown at the bottom of this page.

Not everything here becomes a story. A source appearing on this list means it is watched — not that anything it publishes is reported. Items from unidentified blogs, content farms and unedited press-release wires are discarded even when they surface through one of these monitors.

Sources can be added, edited or switched off in the admin portal.
