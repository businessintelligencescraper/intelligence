Risk

Scale of potential impact — independent of whether we act.
High

It reaches our own products, it's genuinely moving, and there's a window where acting matters.

Expect a named owner and a decision. Typically a briefing, a compliance check, or a position to align before the moment passes.

What pushes an item to High

It's ours It applies to our own products or operations — not a story about another company.
It's moving A vote, a ruling, a signature deadline. Live text with momentum, not an argument someone is making.
There's a window Acting now is worth more than acting later — before a floor vote, before a decision lands.

A deliberate bias toward restraint. The system is instructed not to inflate ratings, because the scale only works if High means something when it appears. Most days are Low and Medium. These are structured judgements rather than a formula — reliable on clear-cut cases, debatable at the margins, and worth challenging when a rating looks wrong.
