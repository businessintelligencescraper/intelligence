# ⚠ High-Risk Alert — September 26, 2026

**Multistate E. coli Outbreak Tied to Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw Milk Cheese Prompts Recall**
FDA and CDC are investigating a multistate E. coli O26:H11 outbreak linked to Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheese (medium and sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, Monterey jack). As of Sept. 25, 13 people across nine states have been sickened, 8 hospitalized, and 3 have developed hemolytic uremic syndrome (HUS); more than half of those sickened are children age 5 or younger. Illness onset dates run July 7–Aug. 26, 2026, and FDA contacted the firm Sept. 24 to request a recall.
*Why it matters:* A severe, multistate outbreak with HUS cases concentrated in young children and national broadcast coverage is a live reminder of the category-wide reputational and regulatory scrutiny cheese-safety incidents draw, even though the implicated product is unpasteurized raw-milk cheese, outside a conventional pasteurized-dairy manufacturer's product line. Expect renewed media and advocacy attention on raw-milk/cheese safety more broadly.
*Recommended action:* Brief food-safety and communications leadership on the outbreak; confirm there is no supply-chain or private-label link to Sierra Nevada Cheese Co.; monitor for further FDA/CDC updates and any raw-milk-cheese policy response.
