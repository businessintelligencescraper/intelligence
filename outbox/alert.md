# ⚠ High-Risk Alert — August 4, 2026

**FDA's Proposed Rule Ending Self-Affirmed GRAS Notification Clears White House Review**
FDA's proposed rule to require mandatory notification for most GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) food-substance determinations completed OMB/OIRA review, per a notice posted July 31, 2026. The rule (RIN 0910-AJ02) would end reliance on purely self-affirmed GRAS status for most substances and require FDA to maintain a public GRAS-notice inventory; the 2026 Unified Regulatory Agenda lists Federal Register publication as anticipated for December 2026.
*Why it matters:* This would be the largest change to the GRAS pathway in decades, affecting nearly every food and dairy ingredient supplier that relies on self-affirmed GRAS determinations for enzymes, cultures, flavors, and functional ingredients. It runs on a parallel federal track to the New York state GRAS-disclosure bill IDFA is already fighting, so both fronts should be briefed together once the proposed rule text and comment period are public.
*Recommended action:* Flag to ingredient/regulatory-affairs teams now; prepare to brief leadership when the proposed rule and comment period publish, expected around December 2026.
