# ⚠ High-Risk Alert — August 25, 2026

**Canada Publishes Retaliatory Tariff List: US Dairy and Cheese Exports Face 25% Duties Starting Sept. 8**
Canada published the full product list and rates for its "dollar for dollar" retaliatory tariffs on Aug. 25, covering more than 700 US goods worth roughly $20 billion. Dairy and cheese products are among the categories hit with a 25% duty (alongside appliances and other goods), steel and aluminum face 50%, and most remaining items are set at 15%; the tariffs take effect Sept. 8. Canada's Prime Minister and Trade Minister confirmed the list after talks with Washington collapsed and US tariffs on Canadian dairy ingredients took effect Aug. 22.
*Why it matters:* This is the first confirmed, quantified rate for the dairy-sector retaliation Canada flagged three days earlier — a 25% tariff on US dairy and cheese exports to Canada, effective in under two weeks. It directly raises costs and reduces competitiveness for any US dairy manufacturer exporting cheese or dairy products north of the border, and it means tariffs are now elevated in both directions between the two countries with no resumed-talks timeline announced.
*Recommended action:* Brief leadership on the confirmed 25% tariff on US dairy/cheese exports to Canada ahead of the Sept. 8 effective date; assess Canada-bound export exposure and pricing implications now that specific rates are public.
