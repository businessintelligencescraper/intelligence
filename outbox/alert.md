# ⚠ High-Risk Alert — September 17, 2026

**USDA Ends Mandatory Dairy-Checkoff Funding of ESG and Net-Zero Programs**
USDA Secretary Brooke Rollins directed the National Dairy Board on Sept. 17 to stop using mandatory dairy-checkoff assessments to fund ESG, net-zero and climate-neutrality initiatives, naming the Innovation Center for U.S. Dairy's Greener Cattle Initiative, U.S. Dairy Net Zero Initiative, Pathways to Dairy Net Zero, Sustainability Alliance and U.S. Dairy Stewardship Commitment, effective immediately. A companion Under Secretary memo extends the same restriction to all USDA commodity checkoff boards, not just dairy. The action follows a June 2026 lawsuit by three Wisconsin dairy farmers, represented by the Wisconsin Institute for Law & Liberty, arguing checkoff funds were being used to subsidize climate advocacy farmers did not support; WILL and the American Dairy Coalition publicly welcomed the action the same day.

*Why it matters:* The Innovation Center for U.S. Dairy's sustainability infrastructure, including FARM Environmental Stewardship reporting that many processors and retail customers reference in their own environmental claims, has relied on checkoff funding. An immediate cutoff with no transition period creates a program-continuity gap and a reputational crosscurrent, since environmentally-minded retail customers and anti-ESG stakeholders will read this action in opposite ways.

*Recommended action:* Brief leadership now: inventory which checkoff-funded sustainability programs (FARM ES, Net Zero Initiative, Stewardship Commitment) are participated in or cited in customer and public materials, and prepare a holding statement before customers, employees or press ask.
