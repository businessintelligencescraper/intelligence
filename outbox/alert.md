# ⚠ High-Risk Alert — October 1, 2026

**FDA Opens Second Outbreak Investigation Also Linked to Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw Milk Cheese**
FDA has opened an investigation into a new multistate Listeria monocytogenes outbreak (11 confirmed illnesses as of the agency's Sept. 30 update) after preliminary interviews found that all nine patients questioned so far reported eating Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheese — the same product already recalled nationwide over a separate E. coli O26:H11 outbreak (13 illnesses, 8 hospitalizations, 3 cases of HUS) first reported in late September. FDA has not named a confirmed food source and says traceback is ongoing.
*Why it matters:* A second distinct pathogen now circumstantially tied to the same recalled raw-milk cheese brand compounds an already serious outbreak and lands inside the Oct. 9 public-comment window on a draft FDA citizen petition seeking mandatory warning labels on all raw-milk cheese; it materially strengthens the case for that petition and raises the odds that aged/raw-milk cheese becomes subject to new category-wide labeling requirements that would reach any dairy processor selling it.
*Recommended action:* Brief food-safety and government-affairs leadership now; confirm there is no supply-chain, private-label or co-packing link to Sierra Nevada Cheese Co.; track the Oct. 9 Marler Clark citizen-petition comment deadline and any CDC/FDA case-count or source updates.
