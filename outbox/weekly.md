# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Weekly Briefing — Week of September 21–27, 2026

## The week in one paragraph

The most consequential development was USDA's Sept. 17 order ending mandatory dairy-checkoff funding for ESG and net-zero programs, an abrupt policy reversal that leaves processors without clarity on sustainability commitments built on that funding. The dairy trade landscape also showed new strain: Dairy Farmers of America, the industry's largest cooperative, withdrew from the International Dairy Foods Association over a unilateral milk-pricing reform petition, exposing a rift between the sector's two largest trade groups. California continued to lead state-level food and environmental regulation, signing the first-in-the-nation "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" food label law and adopting a new statewide order tightening nitrate controls on Central Valley dairy manure. On the federal legislative track, the Senate Agriculture Committee advanced its 2026 Farm Bill on a party-line vote, and the House Energy and Commerce Committee moved forward two bills that would set national standards for packaging recyclability claims. Rounding out the week, a $385 million DOJ settlement with Abbott over contaminated infant formula — paired with dropped criminal charges — renewed scrutiny of how food-safety enforcement decisions get made, and a new screwworm case in New Mexico added a note of uncertainty just before the Santa Teresa, NM port is set to reopen to Mexican cattle imports.

## Top developments

**USDA Ends Mandatory Dairy-Checkoff Funding of ESG and Net-Zero Programs** (High · Executive Awareness Required)
USDA Secretary Brooke Rollins directed the National Dairy Board on Sept. 17 to stop using mandatory dairy-checkoff assessments to fund ESG, net-zero and climate-neutrality initiatives, naming the Innovation Center for U.S. Dairy's Greener Cattle Initiative, U.S. Dairy Net Zero Initiative, Pathways to Dairy Net Zero, Sustainability Alliance and U.S. Dairy Stewardship Commitment, effective immediately; a companion memo extends the restriction to all USDA commodity checkoffs. *Why it matters:* The Innovation Center's FARM Environmental Stewardship reporting, which many processors and retail customers reference in their own environmental claims, has relied on checkoff funding; an immediate cutoff with no transition period creates a program-continuity gap and a reputational crosscurrent, since environmentally-minded retail customers and anti-ESG stakeholders will read this in opposite ways. *Recommended action:* Brief leadership now: inventory which checkoff-funded sustainability programs the company participates in or cites in customer/public materials, and prepare a holding statement. [USDA](https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/press-releases/2026/09/17/usda-ends-mandatory-farmer-funding-esg-commitments-dairy-checkoff), [Dairy Business News](https://dairybusiness.com/adc-welcomes-usda-lawfare-action-including-esg-demands-on-dairy-farmers/)

**DOJ Settles Contaminated Infant-Formula Case With Abbott for $385M, Drops Criminal Charges Amid Political-Donation Scrutiny** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required)
The Justice Department announced Sept. 14 that Abbott will pay nearly $385 million to settle False Claims Act allegations tied to its 2022 Sturgis, Michigan plant shutdown, closing a parallel criminal investigation in favor of the civil settlement — a more lenient outcome than career prosecutors reportedly believed the evidence supported — amid reporting on Abbott's political donations. *Why it matters:* Abbott is an infant-formula maker, not a dairy processor, but this is a live case study in how DOJ is exercising discretion on food-safety criminal enforcement, and any food or infant-nutrition manufacturer facing a serious contamination event should expect this precedent — and the public scrutiny attached to it — to shape enforcement expectations and media framing. *Recommended action:* Brief food-safety/regulatory-affairs leadership on DOJ's enforcement posture; monitor for spillover into broader food-safety-enforcement coverage. [Food Safety Magazine](https://www.food-safety.com/articles/11562-trump-admin-stopped-criminal-charges-against-abbott-nutrition-for-infant-formula-illnesses), [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/09/15/Abbott-infant-formula-investigation-settlement/7471789451929)

**Senate Agriculture Committee Advances Farm Bill 2.0 on 12-11 Party-Line Vote** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required)
The Republican-led Senate Agriculture Committee voted 12-11 on Sept. 16 to advance the Agricultural Act of 2026, reconvening after an Aug. 6 markup failed over an unrelated SNAP dispute; the bill now heads to the full Senate floor with no vote date yet set. *Why it matters:* The bill's dairy title survives intact — reauthorization of the Dairy Forward Pricing Program, Dairy Indemnity Payment Program and Dairy Promotion and Research Board through 2031, plus a new mandatory dairy-manufacturer cost and yield reporting requirement tied to Federal Milk Marketing Order pricing. *Recommended action:* Brief regulatory-affairs leadership that the dairy reporting mandate has cleared committee; no filing action needed until floor scheduling firms up. [Agri-Pulse](https://www.agri-pulse.com/articles/25257-senate-ag-passes-farm-bill-along-party-lines-amid-gop-drama), [Senate Agriculture Committee](https://www.agriculture.senate.gov/agricultural-act-of-2026-farm-bill-20)

**California Governor Signs First-in-Nation 'Non-Ultraprocessed Certified' Food Label Law** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required)
Gov. Gavin Newsom signed AB 2244, creating a voluntary state certification program and "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" seal for foods excluding synthetic dyes, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and certain other processing aids, overseen by the California Department of Public Health with third-party certifiers accredited by June 1, 2029. *Why it matters:* This is the first state law building a certification/labeling program around a legal ultra-processed-food definition; its criteria touch additives, flavor enhancers and processing aids common in flavored milk, cultured dairy and processed cheese, creating both a marketing opportunity and a labeling distinction other states may copy. *Recommended action:* Brief labeling/regulatory affairs; begin assessing which portfolio products could qualify for the voluntary seal. [EWG](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news-release/2026/09/newsom-signs-landmark-california-transparency-bill-creating-non)

**FDA Executive Summary Reveals Clover Hill Dairy's Fatal Listeria Strain Persisted in Plant Since 2021** (Medium · Monitor Only)
FDA published an executive summary of its investigation into the closed Clover Hill Dairy soft-cheese Listeria outbreak (15 sickened, 1 death), showing whole-genome sequencing matched the outbreak strain to environmental and patient samples dating back to 2021. *Why it matters:* A resident Listeria strain went undetected for roughly five years despite routine sanitation and testing before it caused a fatal outbreak, reinforcing the case for rigorous environmental monitoring programs at cheese and soft-dairy facilities. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-listeria-monocytogenes-soft-cheese-june-2026), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/09/fda-releases-executive-summaries-on-two-outbreak-investigations/)

**California Adopts Statewide Order to Curb Dairy Manure Nitrate Pollution in Groundwater** (Medium · Monitor Only)
The California State Water Resources Control Board voted unanimously on Sept. 15 to require Central Valley dairies to meet a 10 mg/L nitrate drinking-water standard, tighten manure storage/land-application practices, and supply alternative drinking water to households with contaminated wells, with final regional rules due within five years. *Why it matters:* California is the top US milk-producing state, and regulators estimate about 94% of Central Valley dairy-related nitrogen loading comes from manure spread on cropland, reshaping farm-level compliance costs across a sourcing region a national dairy manufacturer depends on. [CA Water Board](https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/water_issues/programs/enforcement/dairy_general_order.shtml), [Inside Climate News](https://insideclimatenews.org/news/18092026/california-dairy-manure-groundwater-regulation/)

**New Screwworm Case Confirmed in New Mexico Horse Days Before Santa Teresa Port Reopens to Mexican Cattle** (Medium · Monitor Only)
New Mexico and USDA confirmed Sept. 17 that a horse in Grant County tested positive for New World screwworm — the state's second case since June — just after USDA announced the Santa Teresa, NM port will reopen to Mexican cattle imports on Sept. 24; officials say the case is unrelated and unlikely to delay the reopening. *Why it matters:* Santa Teresa would be the largest port reopened yet in the phased return of Mexican cattle, feeding a cattle and dairy-replacement-heifer supply chain the industry depends on; a fresh US-side detection this close to a reopening date keeps alive the risk of a pause, as a similar detection nearly did in Arizona in August. [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/6097215-new-mexico-screwworm-horse-confirmed/)

**House Energy & Commerce Committee Advances Federal Packaging-Claims Bills to Full House** (Medium · Monitor Only)
The House Energy and Commerce Committee voted Sept. 16 to advance the PACK Act (H.R. 6832) and the Recycled Materials Attribution Act (H.R. 7502) to the full House floor, as part of a bipartisan 16-bill markup; the bills would set federal, FTC-enforced standards for packaging claims like "recyclable" and "recycled content," preempting the current state patchwork. *Why it matters:* Full-committee passage moves a potential federal packaging-claims standard closer to a House floor vote, which would matter for packaging-labeling compliance across inconsistent state EPR and claims laws; the mass-balance recycled-content provision remains contested. [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/policy-legislation/2026/09/18/house-committee-advances-two-packaging-and-recycling-related-bills/)

**Dairy Farmers of America Withdraws From IDFA Over Milk-Pricing Reform Petition** (Medium · Monitor Only)
Dairy Farmers of America, the nation's largest dairy cooperative, is ending its IDFA membership after IDFA proceeded with a USDA petition to change how "make allowances" are set in the Federal Milk Marketing Order system without full membership consensus, saying the move put it in an untenable position relative to its farmer-owners. *Why it matters:* This is a rare, public fracture between the dairy industry's two largest trade associations over federal milk-pricing reform, a process that directly affects processor input costs, and signals the FMMO make-allowance fight is deepening. [Agri-Pulse](https://www.agri-pulse.com/articles/19576-dairy-farmers-of-america-withdraws-from-idfa-over-fmmo-proposal), [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/policy/largest-dairy-cooperative-withdraws-international-dairy-foods-association)

**CoBank Analysis: Ten States Now Produce 74% of US Milk as Production Consolidates** (Low · Monitor Only)
A Sept. 17 CoBank analysis finds 10 states now produce 74% of US milk, with growth concentrated in states with available water and new processing investment, while Wisconsin's herd has shrunk by about 44,000 head since January 2023. *Why it matters:* Continued geographic consolidation of US milk supply has direct implications for sourcing, plant-siting, water-risk and logistics planning as new processing capacity follows the milk. [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/u-s-dairy-map-redrawn-10-states-now-control-74-milk-production-industry-undergoes-historic-s)

**Consumer and Environmental Groups Rally Against FRESH Act Food-Safety Preemption Bill** (Low · Monitor Only)
EWG held a Capitol Hill rally Sept. 15 opposing the FRESH Act (H.R. 8578), which would make FDA the sole regulator of food chemicals and preempt state food-safety laws; the bill remains in House Agriculture Committee with no scheduled markup. *Why it matters:* The bill's preemption clause would directly override several state food-safety laws already on this watch list, so its trajectory affects how much state-level regulatory patchwork risk persists. [EWG](https://www.ewg.org/news-insights/news-release/2026/09/toxics-table-rally-join-ewg-protect-state-food-safety-laws)

**US Dairy Trade Groups Welcome USDA Official's Appointment to Lead UN World Food Programme** (Low · Monitor Only)
NMPF and the US Dairy Export Council welcomed the Sept. 18 announcement that USDA Under Secretary Luke Lindberg will become the next Executive Director of the UN World Food Programme, pending Executive Board consultations. *Why it matters:* A notable, dairy-trade-endorsed personnel change with potential long-term relevance to how US dairy products are positioned in global food-aid and export channels, though no near-term business impact. [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/09/18/world-food-program-un-lindberg-trump/d8977e28-b3b5-11f1-92c2-5c918f4a6127_story.html), [NMPF](https://www.nmpf.org/u-s-dairy-statement-on-luke-lindberg-world-food-programme-appointment/)
