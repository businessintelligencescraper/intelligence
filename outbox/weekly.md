## Top developments

**IDFA Names Matt Herrick Next President and CEO, Succeeding Michael Dykes** (Low · Monitor Only)
IDFA's Executive Council selected Matt Herrick — currently president and CEO of the International Bottled Water Association and a seven-year IDFA veteran — as the association's next president and CEO, succeeding Michael Dykes, who is retiring after a decade leading IDFA. Herrick takes over Jan. 1, 2027, with Dykes staying through Jan. 31 to assist the transition. *Why it matters:* IDFA is the primary national trade association representing US dairy processors on labeling, GRAS, tariff and nutrition-policy fights; a leadership change at the top of that advocacy relationship is worth tracking as the new CEO sets 2027 priorities, though it is a routine, well-managed succession. [Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/agribusiness-news/idfa-selects-matt-herrick-as-next-president-and-ceo), [Dairy Herd Management](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/idfa-announces-matt-herrick-next-president-and-ceo-succeeding-michael-dykes)

**90+ Farm, Labor and Consumer Groups Urge Congress to Advance Dairy Market Stabilization Bill** (Medium · Monitor Only)
A coalition of more than 90 farmer, rural, labor, processor, consumer and environmental organizations, led by the National Family Farm Coalition, sent a letter to Congress urging action on the Milk From Family Dairies Act of 2026 (S.4906), which has sat in the Senate Agriculture Committee without a hearing since it was introduced in June. *Why it matters:* The bill would create a dairy market stabilization program with size-scaled price floors and production quotas — a supply-management structure that would directly reshape how processors source and pay for raw milk if it ever advances; the breadth of this coalition signals building political pressure worth tracking alongside Farm Bill negotiations. [National Family Farm Coalition](https://nffc.net/over-90-farmer-labor-environmental-consumer-groups-demand-fair-dairy-policy-reform/), [FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/10/27/dairy-farmers-push-congress-on-industry-reform/)

**FDA/USDA Criticized for Delayed, Informal Public Naming of Companies Tied to Foodborne Outbreaks** (Medium · Potential Media Interest)
A food-safety trade column reported that USDA confirmed only in an email statement to a reporter — not a press release or updated outbreak page, and roughly a month after first notification — that a retailer's packaged chicken salad was linked to an E. coli outbreak that has sickened at least 50 people. The disclosure follows a citizen petition filed with FDA asking the agency to name firms implicated in outbreaks even when no recall occurs. *Why it matters:* The episode and the pending petition both push toward faster, more public naming of companies tied to outbreaks before any formal recall — a policy shift that would raise reputational exposure for any food or dairy manufacturer swept into a future outbreak investigation. *Recommended action:* Brief crisis-communications leads that FDA/USDA naming practices for outbreak-implicated firms are under pressure to move earlier and more informally. [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/10/publishers-platform-usda-finally-on-the-record-names-trader-joes-in-e-coli-outbreak/), [Marler Blog](https://www.marlerblog.com/case-news/fifty-people-sick-a-known-supplier-and-fda-still-wont-say-its-name-that-is-wrong-and-here-is-how-we-fix-it/)

**NAW Asks Oregon Court to Pause Its Own Packaging-EPR Fee Judgment Pending Appeal** (Low · Monitor Only)
The National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors asked the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon to stay its own judgment — which upheld Oregon's packaging EPR law — while NAW's appeal is pending before the Ninth Circuit. A ruling matters before January 2027, when the next round of EPR invoices goes out. *Why it matters:* If granted, the stay would restore a fee exemption that directly affects Oregon EPR compliance costs for any packaging supplier to a national dairy manufacturer that is an NAW member; if denied, members must decide whether to pay invoices they may not get refunded even if the law is later struck down on appeal. [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/10/20/naw-asks-to-pause-oregon-epr-fees/)
