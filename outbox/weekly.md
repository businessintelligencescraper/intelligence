# Corporate Affairs Intelligence — Weekly Briefing — Week of October 5–11, 2026

## The week in one paragraph

California became the first state to put a government-run "ultra-processed" certification on the shelf, signing AB 2244 to create a voluntary "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" seal for products free of synthetic dyes, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and certain processing aids — with no compliance deadline before 2029, but a clear template other states are expected to copy. On trade, the long-flagged US ban on Canadian-origin whey and other dairy ingredients took effect as scheduled on September 29 with no last-minute deal, while a newly created US-China Board of Trade named dairy among the products it will consider for tariff relief, though no actual tariff cut has happened yet. The Sierra Nevada Graziers raw-milk cheese story continued to evolve: a second, Listeria-linked outbreak investigation that initially appeared tied to the same recalled cheese was then walked back by FDA as unconfirmed, even as a food-safety attorney's push for mandatory warning labels on all raw-milk cheese moves toward an October 9 filing deadline. Elsewhere, California vetoed a bill that would have tightened recycled-content marketing claims, a new industry-backed analysis made the business case for one federal packaging-recycling standard instead of a growing state-by-state patchwork, and a Stanford study found funding and staffing gaps threaten California's separate school ultra-processed-food phase-out.

## Top developments

**US Import Ban on Canadian Dairy, Alcohol and Motorcycles Takes Effect as Scheduled** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required)
The Section 338 import ban President Trump proclaimed on September 8 took effect at 12:01 a.m. ET on September 29, barring importation of Canadian-origin whey protein concentrates, dried whey, fluid whey, modified whey and other affected dairy products, with no USMCA exemption and no last-minute deal announced. *Why it matters:* It removes Canadian-origin whey as a sourcing option outright, compounding an existing 50% tariff on other Canadian dairy ingredients and Canada's own retaliatory tariffs on US dairy exports, with no resolution timeline announced. [AP News via News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/09/29/from-motorcycles-to-booze-us-ban-on-1-billion-worth-of-canadian-imports-goes-into-effect/), [Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/policy/u-s-bans-imports-of-canadian-dairy-products)

**California Governor Signs AB 2244, Creating First-in-Nation "Non-Ultraprocessed Certified" Food Label** (Medium · Executive Awareness Required)
Gov. Gavin Newsom signed AB 2244 on September 28, establishing a voluntary certification seal for products that exclude synthetic dyes, flavor enhancers, non-sugar sweeteners and certain processing aids, overseen by the California Department of Public Health with third-party certifier accreditation required by June 2029. *Why it matters:* It is the first state-run certification built around a legal ultra-processed-food definition, directly touching flavored dairy and processed cheese formulations, and is likely to be copied by other states even though participation is voluntary and there is no near-term compliance deadline. [Office of Governor Gavin Newsom](http://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/28/governor-newsom-signs-legislation-creating-non-upf-label-other-health-bills-advancing-californias-nation-leading-healthcare-strategy/), [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/945875660/governor-newsom-signs-legislation-creating-non-upf-label-other-health-bills-advancing-california-s-nation-leading-healthcare-strategy)

**FDA's New Listeria Investigation Briefly Linked, Then Unlinked, to Sierra Nevada Graziers Raw-Milk Cheese** (High → Medium on correction · Executive Awareness Required)
FDA opened a new multistate Listeria investigation (11 illnesses) that initial coverage reported was circumstantially tied to the same Sierra Nevada Graziers raw-milk cheese already under nationwide recall for a separate E. coli outbreak (13 illnesses, 3 with hemolytic uremic syndrome); follow-up reporting then clarified FDA has named no food, company or state for the Listeria investigation, meaning the apparent second-pathogen link was not confirmed. *Why it matters:* The correction narrows, without eliminating, the urgency behind a pending FDA citizen petition for raw-milk-cheese warning labels, since that petition's case had leaned on the apparent dual-pathogen link; the underlying E. coli outbreak and recall are unchanged. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/investigations-foodborne-illness-outbreaks), [Food Safety News](https://www.foodsafetynews.com/2026/10/new-listeria-outbreak-under-investigation/), [Food Poison Journal](https://www.foodpoisonjournal.com/food-poisoning-information/fda-opened-a-new-listeria-outbreak-investigation-on-september-30-2026-11-sick-no-food-named-no-states-listed-here-is-what-pregnant-women-older-adults-and-everyone-else-should-know/)

**Food-Safety Attorney Opens Public Comment on Draft FDA Petition for Raw Milk Cheese Warning Labels** (Medium · Monitor Only)
Marler Clark opened public comment on a draft citizen petition asking FDA to require a warning statement on all cheese made from unpasteurized milk, following the Sierra Nevada Graziers E. coli outbreak; comments are due October 9 before the firm files with FDA. *Why it matters:* A formal petition seeking a mandatory warning label on an entire product category would set a new labeling precedent for any processor selling aged raw-milk cheese. [PR Newswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/public-comment-sought-on-draft-fda-petition-to-require-a-warning-label-on-raw-milk-cheese--all-comments-due-by-october-9-302892245.html), [Marler Blog](https://www.marlerblog.com/case-news/raw-milk-cheese-needs-a-warning-label-here-is-the-fda-petition-i-plan-to-file-read-it-and-tell-me-what-i-got-wrong-before-i-do/)

**US Dairy Groups Press New US-China Board of Trade, Which Then Names Dairy for Possible Tariff Relief** (Medium → Low · Monitor Only)
NMPF and the US Dairy Export Council urged the newly launched US-China Board of Trade to eliminate China's retaliatory tariffs on US dairy; days later, the US and China issued formal Board procedures naming cheese, butter, yogurt and whey among products under review for reciprocal tariff relief. *Why it matters:* This is the first concrete procedural step toward possibly reopening a market where whey once made up roughly half of China's dairy import volume, but no actual tariff reduction has occurred yet. [NMPF](https://www.nmpf.org/u-s-dairy-statement-on-u-s-china-board-of-trade/), [Cheese Reporter](https://cheesereporter.com/news/global-trade-tariffs/2026/10/01/us-china-establish-trade-board-will-consider-reduced-tariffs-on-us-dairy-exports/)

**California Governor Vetoes AB 2253, the "Protecting Consumers Against Greenwashing Act"** (Low · Monitor Only)
Newsom vetoed a bill that would have tightened recycled-content marketing-claim requirements for packaging, citing the compliance burden on manufacturers amid existing trade and tariff pressure. *Why it matters:* A modest compliance relief for packaged-food manufacturers using plastic tubs, cartons and cups; it does not affect California's broader SB 54 packaging EPR law. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/california-governor-gavin-newsom-vetoes-ab2253-recycled-content/831481/), [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/28/newsom-nixes-recycled-content-claims-bill/)

**WWF and SC Johnson Release Economic Case for a Federal Packaging EPR Framework** (Low · Monitor Only)
A WWF/SC Johnson-commissioned analysis estimates a single federal extended producer responsibility framework could cut local waste-management costs by roughly half versus the current seven-state patchwork, while also halving producer compliance costs. *Why it matters:* Adds a business-backed argument for a federal EPR standard that would set uniform compliance costs and design requirements for packaging nationwide instead of state by state. [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/federal-epr-packaging-push-world-wildlife-fund-sc-johnson/831753/)

**Stanford Study: California's School Ultra-Processed Food Phase-Out Faces Budget and Staffing Hurdles** (Low · Monitor Only)
A Stanford Medicine study of California food-service directors found tight per-meal reimbursement, part-time staffing and vendor shortages are the main barriers to the state's 2029–2035 school UPF phase-out law. *Why it matters:* The first real-world feasibility look at a statutory school UPF phase-out could shape amendments in California and influence how other states calibrate similar bills. [U.S. News & World Report](https://www.usnews.com/news/health-news/articles/2026-09-28/eliminating-ultra-processed-food-from-school-cafeterias-will-be-costly-complicated-study-warns), [Medical Xpress](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-09-ultra-food-school-menus-tricky.html)

**USDA's $70M School-Kitchen Equipment Grants Draw Criticism as Too Small for the "Real Food" Push** (Low · Monitor Only)
School nutrition directors said USDA's $70 million in kitchen-equipment grants, meant to support the administration's "real food" school-meal push, falls well short of what districts need for scratch cooking. *Why it matters:* Shows the MAHA-aligned school-meal push translating into a likely under-resourced procurement program; school-channel dairy formats are directly affected by how much infrastructure districts can build. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/politics/trump-school-lunch-rfk-jr), [WSAW](https://wsaw.com/2026/09/29/trump-administration-is-pushing-real-food-school-here-are-obstacles-many-districts-face)

**Trump Nominates Greg Ibach for USDA Trade Under Secretary Post** (Low · Monitor Only)
Trump nominated former USDA official Greg Ibach to lead USDA's Trade and Foreign Agricultural Affairs office, succeeding Luke Lindberg; NMPF and USDEC welcomed the nomination. *Why it matters:* Signals continuity in USDA's dairy-trade leadership at a moment of active trade disputes with Canada and China, though confirmation carries no near-term business impact on its own. [NMPF](https://www.nmpf.org/u-s-dairy-statement-on-greg-ibach-usda-under-secretary-nomination/), [Oklahoma Farm Report](https://www.oklahomafarmreport.com/2026/09/29/trump-nominates-greg-ibach-to-lead-usda-trade-and-foreign-agricultural-affairs/)

**NAW's Oregon Packaging-EPR Appeal Formally Docketed at Ninth Circuit** (Low · Monitor Only)
The National Association of Wholesaler-Distributors' appeal of the ruling upholding Oregon's packaging EPR law was docketed at the Ninth Circuit, with NAW's opening brief due December 17. *Why it matters:* Sets the timeline for the next phase of the first full federal trial testing the constitutionality of a state packaging-EPR law, a bellwether for similar laws in six other states. [Resource Recycling](http://resource-recycling.com/policy-now/2026/09/25/naw-appeals-oregon-epr-ruling/), [The National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ninth-circuit-review-constitutional-challenge-oregons-epr-law)

**IDFA's Top Regulatory Affairs Executive Announces Retirement; Successor Named** (Low · Monitor Only)
Roberta Wagner, IDFA's senior vice president of regulatory and scientific affairs, will retire in February 2027 after four decades in food regulatory roles; Dr. Samuel Alcaine will succeed her. *Why it matters:* Wagner has been a key industry contact on FDA/USDA dairy regulatory matters; the multi-month transition is worth tracking for continuity on active dockets. [Perishable News](https://perishablenews.com/dairy/idfa-senior-vice-president-roberta-wagner-announces-retirement-samuel-alcaine-to-succeed-wagner/), [RRFN](https://www.rrfn.com/2026/10/02/idfas-wagner-announces-retirement-plan/)
