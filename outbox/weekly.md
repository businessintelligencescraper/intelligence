## Top developments

**Family Farmstead Dairy Recalls Raw Cheddar Cheese Over E. coli Contamination** (Medium · Potential Media Interest)
A small New York dairy voluntarily recalled one lot of raw cheddar cheese on Oct. 15 after testing detected Shiga toxin-producing E. coli; the product was distributed only to New York and New Jersey grocers, and the company has received no illness reports. *Why it matters:* This is the latest in a string of raw-milk cheese safety incidents this year and adds to the pattern advocates are citing in pushing for category-wide raw-milk cheese warning labeling, though it is small in scale and swiftly contained. *Recommended action:* Monitor for whether this gets folded into broader raw-milk-cheese-safety coverage; no direct action needed. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/raw-cheddar-cheese-voluntary-product-recall), [Food Poisoning Bulletin](https://foodpoisoningbulletin.com/2026/family-farmstead-raw-mild-cheddar-recalled-for-e-coli-o157h7/)

**FDA Finalizes Rule Allowing Salt Substitutes in 139 Standardized Foods, Including Cheese and Sour Cream** (Low · Potential Media Interest)
FDA published a final rule on Oct. 13 amending 139 food standards of identity — including dairy standards covering cheeses and sour cream — to permit salt substitutes, where previously only margarine and low-sodium cheddar/Colby had that flexibility. *Why it matters:* This directly answers a multi-year industry request and gives cheese and sour cream makers a reformulation path to cut sodium without losing compliance; both industry and public-health groups welcomed it, lowering reputational risk. The dairy-specific provisions have their own objection window (closing ~Nov. 12) and the rule itself takes effect ~Dec. 12. *Recommended action:* Monitor for reformulation announcements or press pickup tying this to broader sodium-reduction goals. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-issues-final-rule-salt-substitutes-standardized-foods), [FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/10/13/fda-allows-salt-substitutes-in-139-standardized-foods/)

**APHIS Confirms First HPAI Cases in Washington Dairy Herds** (Low · Monitor Only)
USDA confirmed highly pathogenic avian influenza in two Washington State dairy herds on Oct. 8 — the state's first livestock detections — after the herds showed clinical signs despite previously testing negative. *Why it matters:* Dairy cattle in 19 states have now been affected since the outbreak began in 2024; USDA says its eradication strategy is unchanged and the milk supply remains safe, but the expanding footprint keeps biosecurity and herd-sourcing monitoring relevant for any processor with ties to affected regions. [Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/dairy/hpai-confirmed-in-2-washington-dairy-herds), [Dairy Herd](https://www.dairyherd.com/news/new-hpai-cases-washington-put-dairy-farm-biosecurity-back-focus)

**USDA Imposes New 25% Cost-Share Mandate on Dairy Business Innovation Grant Program** (Low · Monitor Only)
A Wisconsin cheesemakers' trade group publicly asked USDA to reverse a newly imposed 25% matching-fund requirement on a USDA dairy grant program that has awarded more than $106 million to 911 dairy businesses across 41 states since 2018, warning the host organizations lack the funds to cover the match. *Why it matters:* The program mainly supports smaller and mid-size processors rather than directly affecting a national manufacturer's own operations, but it's a fresh sign of USDA tightening dairy-sector support funding this year. [Urban Milwaukee](https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=1004400), [WisPolitics](https://www.wispolitics.com/?p=429023)
